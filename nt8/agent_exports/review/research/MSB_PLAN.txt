PRE-REGISTERED: MARKET-STRUCTURE BREAKS (BOS / CHoCH) - DOES THE BREAK'S DIRECTION PREDICT THE NEXT MOVE?
Written Tue 2026-11-03 ~09:40 ET, before any figure has been computed. Live strategy unchanged. Companion to the new chart
indicator MarketStructure.cs (staged\20261103_market_structure): the indicator draws the breaks; this test grades the claim that
the structure break tells "which way it will go".

WHY  Sashkin: "indicator to track when market structure breaks and what way it'd go". The classic price-action / smart-money
reading: a close beyond the latest confirmed swing high is a bullish break (BOS = continuation when the structure is already
bullish, CHoCH = change of character when it was bearish); mirror for swing lows. The claim is that price then continues in the
break's direction. Not tested here before in this form: STRUCTURE (10-20) tested ICT Silver Bullet, liquidity sweeps, Dalton open
types, the initial balance and the 80% rule; RULEMINE and SOURCES2 tested breakout levels (opening range, prior day, Donchian-type)
but never swing-point structure with BOS/CHoCH state. STRUCTURE_LIT (10-31) found no structure READ that predicts the day; this is
the last common one not yet graded.
PAYER  traders positioned against the swing (stops resting beyond the swing high/low) who must cover when it breaks, and
breakout followers; if that liquidity is already consumed by the time the 5-minute close confirms the break, there is no payer.

THE RULE (exactly the indicator's logic, ported bar for bar in msb.py; parity of the swing rule asserted against an independent
vectorised computation)
  Bars: 5-minute, all sessions (ETH), as on the chart. Swing strength N bars: bar c is a swing HIGH when its high is strictly above
  the N older bars' highs and at or above the N newer bars' highs (ties go to the earlier bar); swing LOW mirrored. A swing is
  CONFIRMED only at the close of bar c+N (nothing repaints). The latest confirmed swing high/low that has not been crossed is the
  pending level; a newer confirmed swing replaces it. At every bar close: close > pending high -> bullish break (BOS if the state is
  bullish, CHoCH if bearish or undefined), state becomes bullish, the level is retired; close < pending low mirrored. If both break
  in one bar (possible when a lower swing high sits below a swing low), the bar's own direction (close vs open) decides, both retire.
  Variant "wicks": the bar's high/low instead of the close.

DATA (the project's standard harness)  discovery NQ 2010-06..2019-05 (nq_2010-2019_5m.csv), holdout MNQ NinjaTrader bars
  2019-05..2026-10 (nt_analyzer_bars_to_2026-10-14.csv), secondary holdout MNQ Databento (mnq_db_5m_2019-2026.csv), second instrument
  MES Databento (regime_work\bars5m\mes_5m.csv). First 30 sessions of every file excluded.
COSTS  one tick + $0.50 per side at today's price (scaling_daily attrs_p_ref, the convention of every study here): MNQ and NQ in
  MNQ micro dollars ($2/pt); MES with its own tick/commission at its own price ($5/pt). Zero-cost diagnostic reported.

T1  EVENT STUDY (the direction call).  Every break (all hours), forward return from the break bar's close to the close h bars later,
  signed by the break's direction; h = 6 / 12 / 24 bars (30 min / 1 h / 2 h); events kept non-overlapping (greedy, at least h bars
  apart) and inside one session date. Report n, mean in bps and MNQ ticks, hit rate with a 95% CI, t; split BOS vs CHoCH; also
  RTH-only events (signal bar closes 09:35-15:30). DIRECTION CALL CONFIRMED if, at h = 12, the hit rate's 95% CI excludes 50% on the
  same side on discovery AND holdout, AND the mean forward move exceeds the round-trip cost (2 x per-side) on both.
T2  TRADING RULE (Bulenox-legal).  Signal = a break whose bar closes 09:35-15:30 ET; enter in the break's direction at the next
  bar's open; exit at the close h = 12 bars later or at the 16:00 close, whichever first; one position at a time (signals while in a
  trade are ignored); no overnight. Daily net return per session (0 on sessions without a trade); $/yr at 1 micro, t on sessions,
  trades/yr, win rate, by calendar year, drop-5 best trades, zero cost, 2 ticks.
PRIMARY TRIAL  N = 5, close breaks, h = 12 (the indicator's defaults). EDGE if discovery t >= 2.5 (multiplicity: this program is past
  ten trials) AND holdout t >= 2.0 with the same sign AND Databento MNQ and MES agree in sign AND at most one losing calendar year in
  each window AND discovery t >= 2.0 without its 5 best trades AND positive after cost. Anything else = DEAD.
REPORTED, NOT GATED (robustness, each read counts toward multiplicity; a variant that passes while the primary fails is NOT adopted)
  N in {3, 10}; wick breaks; h in {6, 24}; BOS-only and CHoCH-only; the state alone (hold the bias direction all RTH, flip on each
  break - the "trade with the structure" version).
AUDIT  n of events asserted in the band 0.3-15 per session (a rule that matches nothing fails loudly); fills at the NEXT bar's open;
  no bool.shift; no external series; swing parity loop-vs-vectorised asserted on every file; the primary's trade list spot-checked by
  hand on one printed session.
FILES  msb.py -> msb_output.txt; verdict in MSB_RESULTS.txt. Trial count: 1 gated + 8 reported variants.
