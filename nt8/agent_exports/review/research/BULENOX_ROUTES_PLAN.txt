date (Sunday evening for Monday) - what a 5-minute NinjaTrader strategy gets from the first bar of the new Bulenox day. Exit = the open
of the session's 09:30 bar.

 T1 FOMC night leg: long 1 micro, 18:05 entry on the evening before each FOMC announcement day -> 09:30 open of the announcement day.
    Payer: as for the validated FOMC-eve hold - holders cutting equity risk ahead of the rate decision (pre-FOMC drift, Lucca-Moench
    2015). Hypothesis: most of the 16:00 -> 09:30 premium accrues after the 18:00 reopen. Expected n 120-140; assert.
 T2 FOMC morning: long 1 micro on each announcement day from the 09:30 open to the 13:55 open (12:25 on the eight 2011-2012
    press-conference days, whose statement came at 12:30). Intraday - legal on Bulenox today. Payer: the same pre-announcement drift
    (Lucca-Moench measure it up to the statement). Expected n 125-135; assert.
 T3 Overnight premium, legal leg: C15's rule (session k's RTH close > the 200-day average of RTH closes, joined NQ/MNQ series as in
    src2) -> long 1 micro from the 18:05 entry that evening to the 09:30 open of session k+1. Payer: the overnight risk premium
    (Boyarchenko-Larsen-Whelan 2023, Cliff-Cooper-Gulen 2008). Expected n 2,500-3,100; assert.
