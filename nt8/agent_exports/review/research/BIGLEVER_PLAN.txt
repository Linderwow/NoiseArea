DATA AND MODEL: NoiseArea 1 micro, 16 years at today's cost (scaling_inputs\scaling_daily.csv r_today x N_USD, the Part B input);
m micros = m x that series (costs scale with size). Daily closes only: end-of-day drawdowns are exact on closes; a daily loss
limit is applied as a cap on the day's loss (optimistic: the real stop can trigger intraday and lock in a loss the close would have
recovered - stated, and it matters more at larger sizes). Funded phase: 20,000 bootstrap paths x 504 sessions (21-session month
blocks). Evaluation: 20,000 bootstrap windows of 21 sessions (30 calendar days). Payout requests as soon as eligible, 3 sessions
before the next cycle counts (as bulenox_routes.master).
SELECTION BIAS CONTROL: sizes are chosen on bootstrap seed 20261021 and every reported figure comes from a DIFFERENT seed
(20261022); neighbours of the chosen size are reported (a plateau, not a spike, is required for a recommendation).

GRID (fixed now)
 Evaluation micros q in {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10} (qualification route and Momentum qualification);
 qualification-route resets: R0 never (a failed window = a new $ fee next month), R1 reset for $78 after a breach while >= 5
 sessions of the window remain (unlimited resets).
 Funded micros m in {1, 2, 3, 4} (Master, Fast Track, Momentum Master). All four account sizes.

OUTPUTS per route x account size: P(pass per window) and expected fees per funded account at each q; funded-phase P(open after 1 / 2
years), expected payouts to Sashkin in 2 years, P(any payout) at each m; the combined value per account slot =
E[2-year payouts at m] - E[all fees to get the account at q]; cash needed for the first try; chance the first try ends funded.
Reported against today's plan of record: Qualification 25K at 1 micro / Master at 1 micro (BULENOX_ROUTES Part B: about +$1,550).
DECISION RULE: a route is called BETTER only if its combined value beats the plan of record by >= 50% on the reporting seed AND
its chosen q and m sit on a plateau (both neighbours within 20% of the chosen value). This is a business decision for Sashkin (it
costs money), not a strategy change; the brief's sizing gate (1 micro on live money until 60 clean sessions) is reported beside
any m > 1 result, never overridden here.

AMENDMENT 1 (2026-10-21 17:18 ET - AFTER the first run, declared as such; plan saved 17:13:57, first run's output 17:15:59, kept as
biglever_output_closeonly.txt). The first run chose 10 micros in every evaluation and 4 funded micros - the corner of the grid where
the daily-close model is most optimistic, because Bulenox stops an account INTRADAY: the daily loss limit "includes realized and
unrealized P&L", and the drawdown closes the account when the account value falls below the threshold during the session. The
first run's statement "end-of-day drawdowns are exact on closes" was therefore wrong for breaches. Fix, applied to every route and
both seeds with no other change: intraday_worst.py rebuilds the same 16-year series (r_close equals nav_lab.series exactly) and adds
each session's worst point (realized + open P&L at each 5-minute bar's low for longs / high for shorts, the whole day's costs
charged). Each simulated day at m micros: worst = m x r_min; with a daily loss limit, a worst point at or below -DLL ends the day at
-DLL (the stop's own slippage ignored); the account is closed if the balance before the day + worst < threshold; otherwise the day
closes at m x r_close (or -DLL) and the end-of-day threshold moves on closes as before. The close-only figures stay reported as
the optimistic bound. Selection (pick seed / report seed / plateau / 50% rule) unchanged. Bar highs and lows contain every
intrabar extreme, so the worst point is as exact as the 5-minute bars; what stays optimistic is the stop's fill (assumed exactly
at the limit, no slippage).
