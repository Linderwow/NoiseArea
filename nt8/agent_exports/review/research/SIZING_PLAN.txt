PRE-REGISTERED SIZING TEST  (written 2026-10-15, before running it)

Strategy: published base case exactly as in NoiseAreaStrategy (VM 1.0, 14 days, checks 10:00..15:30 every 30 min, paper trail).
Only the number of contracts per day changes. Signals, entries, exits, costs unchanged (1 tick + $0.50 per side per contract).

Daily volatility proxy known before the open: sig_day[k] = mean over the previous 14 valid days of |Close(16:00)/Open(9:30) - 1|
  (this is the noise-area sigma at the 16:00 slot, already computed by the indicator).
Contracts[k] = clip( round( c0 / sig_day[k] ), 1, 3 )   with c0 = median of sig_day over MNQ 2019-2022 (fixed thereafter).
  Floor 1 because a micro cannot be split; cap 3 as a prop-account-sized limit. Expected average ~1.3 contracts.

Comparison: fixed 1 contract (current) vs. volatility-targeted, on
  D  MNQ 2019-2022 (where c0 is set)     V1 MNQ 2023-2025     V3 NinjaTrader MNQ 2026     (V2 MES reported, not scored: no edge there)
Metrics: t-stat of daily P&L, net, max drawdown, net / max drawdown, average contracts.
PASS (adopt in the NinjaScript as an option, default still 1 contract until Sim101 gates pass):
  vol-targeted t-stat > fixed t-stat on D AND on V1 AND on V3, and net/maxDD not worse on V1.
No other sizing rule will be tried after seeing the results.
