MARKET-STRUCTURE READING AND "APPLY THE EDGE THAT FITS THE STRUCTURE" - EVIDENCE REVIEW   (2026-10-30/31; five web scouts, ~125
searches/fetches, primary sources opened where possible; each family mapped to what this project has already tested, with the status)

THE QUESTION (Sashkin): read the market's structure (the day type, the auction, the flow) early in the session and run the strategy
that fits it - trend-following on trend days, fading on balance days - the way Patrick's Golden Gate is described. What is the
mechanism behind each kind of "structure read", what evidence exists that it works in advance, and what is left to test here?

BOTTOM LINE
  1. There is exactly ONE structure variable with a documented mechanism and peer-reviewed, multi-market, out-of-sample evidence for
     trend days versus reversal days in index futures: option dealers' hedging demand (gamma). Its footprint is visible in NoiseArea's
     own P&L (3-4x more per day on low-gamma days, every split). It is NOT usable as a switch (gamma_switch.py: every reversion family
     loses in every gamma state), not as a filter and not as a sizing rule (GEX_RESULTS), and its free data source has closed.
  2. Every other "structure read" - Market Profile day/open types, initial balance, the 80% rule, ICT/Wyckoff liquidity, Crabel
     compression, VWAP structure, chart analogs, image CNNs, HMM regimes, breadth internals, order flow at the open - is either
     untested in the literature (descriptive statistics with no out-of-sample P&L), tested and null (Mesfin 2026 on MNQ; Chordia et
     al.: flow predictability dies in 5-60 minutes), or tested here and dead (STRUCTURE, REGIME x3, WEEKEND A1/A3/A2, GAMMA_SWITCH).
  3. The intraday regularities that DO replicate out of sample are all "price so far -> the last half hour" (Gao 2018, Baltussen 2021):
     R2 1-3%, Sharpe 1.7 before costs across 60 markets - and on Nasdaq futures 2010-2026 that rule earns $467/yr per micro (t 0.6)
     and has lost money every year since 2023 (lh_test.py). The robust structure effect in the literature is the one NoiseArea
     already harvests in a stronger form (a move beyond the noise band, held to the close).
  4. The honest reading of "Patrick's way": the day type is worth 2-3x NoiseArea in hindsight; nothing on hand or in the literature
     calls it in advance better than a coin flip; the only open test is his own time-stamped calls (patrick_calls.py).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FAMILY BY FAMILY  (mechanism | best public evidence | tested here | status)

1. DEALER GAMMA / HEDGING DEMAND  (the mechanism that is real)
   Mechanism: dealers short gamma must buy as the market rises and sell as it falls (they chase -> trends persist, especially into the
   close); dealers long gamma do the opposite (they dampen -> reversals). Leveraged ETFs rebalance the same way at the close.
   Evidence: Baltussen, Da, Lammers, Martens, JFE 142 (2021) - 60+ futures 1974-2020; the day-so-far return predicts the last half
   hour, pooled OOS R2 2.88%, equity-index long-short Sharpe 1.73 pre-cost, hit 55%; on S&P futures 1996-2020 the effect exists only on
   negative-net-gamma days (beta 6.63, t 4.78) and vanishes on positive-gamma days (t 1.03). Barbon & Buraschi (2019/21): one s.d. more
   dealer gamma -> intraday autocorrelation 1.5-2 points lower, daily range 16 bp lower (in-sample panel). Ni-Pearson-Poteshman-White
   (RFS 2021): hedging explains ~10-13% of daily |returns| in single stocks. 0DTE era (2023-2026): the papers disagree even on the sign
   of the volatility effect (Dim-Eraker-Vilkov: lower; Brogaard-Han-Won: higher; Cboe: nil at <= 0.2-1.9% of liquidity); none gives
   an ex-ante day-type signal; a 2026 Princeton thesis finds the gamma channel adds no predictive power for intraday variance.
   FlashAlpha's 8-year SPY study: GEX vs next-day realized vol rho -0.36 raw, -0.03 after VIX and ATM IV - "GEX is VIX in disguise".
   Tested here: GEX_RESULTS (10-16): mechanism footprint PASSES (low-gamma days earn 2-5x more per day, both sources, both periods);
   filters and sizing FAIL. GAMMA_SWITCH (10-30): as the strategy switch, DEAD - reversion families lose in every gamma state; the
   sign cell flips between periods (GEX < 0: +$56/day FIT, -$19 VALIDATE, +$59 HOLDOUT); chosen switch $4,660 vs $6,285/yr, P 0.66.
   Data reality: SqueezeMetrics' free DIX/GEX CSV is gone (404; plan $720/month); FlashAlpha $1,199/month; OptionMetrics academic.
   gex_dix.csv ends 2026-10-16 for good; a forward GEX series could be built daily from Cboe delayed chains (gex-tracker-style scripts,
   free) but any test must control for VIX. Status: CLOSED as a rule; kept as the diagnostic it is.

2. "PRICE SO FAR -> LAST HALF HOUR" (intraday momentum papers)
   Gao, Han, Li, Zhou, JFE 2018: SPY first half hour predicts the last half hour, OOS R2 1.8-2.7%, 6.3%/yr gross; CXO replication
   2012-14 r 0.12, "too small after frictions". Baltussen 2021 (above): the rest-of-day version is the stronger one; when the first
   half hour and the rest of the day disagree, the first-half-hour coefficient turns NEGATIVE - early reads are overridden by later
   price. Rosa 2022 (J. Futures Markets): overnight -> last half hour fails out of sample.
   Tested here: STRUCTURE (O) Gao's first-half-hour signal on NQ/MNQ: -$683/yr holdout; XMKT: last-half-hour momentum on 7 other
   markets: dead; LH (10-30): Baltussen's exact rest-of-day rule on NQ 2010-19 + MNQ 2019-26: $467/yr, Sharpe 0.16, t +0.63, 7 of 17
   years positive, by year 2020 +$7,700 / 2022 +$5,914 then 2023 -$1,031, 2024 -$3,887, 2025 -$6,354, 2026 -$1,335; as an addition
   on NoiseArea's flat-at-15:30 days it LOWERS the Sharpe (1.00 vs 1.09, P 0.79). The effect is positive only when NoiseArea is already
   in the position (+0.50 pts/trade, 100% same side) and negative when it is flat (-0.17). Observation, post hoc and NOT a rule: since
   2023 the Nasdaq's last half hour has REVERSED the day's move - consistent with the 0DTE-era notes (retail leaning against the move)
   and with Safari-Schmidhuber 2025 (sub-hour horizons sit in the reversion regime). It can only be tested forward; it is not tested.
   Status: CLOSED. NoiseArea is the costed, stronger form of this effect (it needs the move to exceed the noise band first).

3. AUCTION MARKET THEORY / MARKET PROFILE  (day types, open types, initial balance, value area, 80% rule)
   Mechanism claimed: the market "auctions" to find value; an open outside value with conviction (open-drive) means other-timeframe
   participants are active -> trend day; an open inside value -> rotation. No academic test of TPO day-type or open-type labels exists
   (five scouts, none found). The only numbers: Firich 2012 (contract/period unstated): trend days 9.5% of days, IB broken on 74-78%
   of days, next-day trend probability 9.5-10.4% whatever today's type (no persistence); TradingStats 2026 (ES 2,686 days, no costs,
   no out-of-sample, one table sums over 100%): 97.8% of days break the IB, a C-period close outside the IB -> 79-82% reach a 50%
   extension and 46-50% a 100% extension, a retrace under 25% after the break -> 94% close in the break direction; median MFE:MAE
   from the break 1.03-1.17 = no edge after costs; ORB expectancy -0.28 pts before costs. The 80% rule measures ~62% wherever anyone
   counted (futures.io 120 setups; a TradeStation test), triggers a handful of ES days a year, and has no published sample.
   Tested here: STRUCTURE (10-20) coded Dalton open types, IB breakout/fade, the 80% rule, value-area rules, as setups and as states:
   open type t +1.19 holdout, IB fade -$399/yr, 80% rule $159/yr, no switcher beats NoiseArea (0 of 28). The 684-variant sweep and
   STRUCTURE covered ORB. Status: CLOSED. NoiseArea's band IS an initial-balance-type rule with a statistically fitted width.

4. ICT / SMART MONEY / WYCKOFF  (liquidity sweeps, fair value gaps, Silver Bullet, springs)
   Mechanism claimed: institutions run stops beyond obvious levels, then reverse. No academic evidence of any kind; Chordia-Roll-
   Subrahmanyam (JFE 2005): order-imbalance predictability in large stocks lasts 5-60 minutes, FTSE futures ~10 minutes.
   Tested here: STRUCTURE (M, N): Silver Bullet -$3,046/yr discovery (all 9 variants lost), liquidity sweep/spring -$1,053/yr
   holdout with a $13,859 drawdown. Status: CLOSED.

5. CRABEL / VOLATILITY COMPRESSION  (NR4, NR7, inside day, ORB)
   Mechanism: volatility clusters; after compression, expansion. True for range SIZE (opening-range size vs remaining range rho ~0.59
   in Astreka's development sample; the bands already scale with it), not for DIRECTION (opening-range direction hit rate 3.7 points
   above 50% with a CI that includes 50%). Tested here: STRUCTURE (P, T1): Crabel ORB after an inside day $891/yr holdout, t 0.74;
   compression as a state: NoiseArea did WORSE after compression days (Sharpe 0.23 vs 1.00) - the opposite of the folklore.
   Status: CLOSED.

6. CHART READING BY MACHINE  (image CNNs, chart analogs)
   Jiang, Kelly, Xiu, J. Finance 2023: a CNN on OHLC chart images, trained once on 1993-2000, tested 2001-2019: per-stock accuracy
   52.5-53.6% (momentum 52.1%), decile long-short Sharpe 2.4 equal-weight / 0.5 value-weight monthly; much of the gain is the
   cross-sectional min-max rescaling (a linear model on image-scaled data gets Sharpe 2.0); the paper never times a single asset
   (its own heuristic: 52% -> 53% accuracy moves a timing Sharpe from 0.6 to 0.8). Murray-Xia-Xiao JFE 2024: cross-section again.
   Single-asset "candle CNN" papers report 90-99% accuracies - the signature of label leakage. Replications: BSIC reduced version
   Sharpe 0.42 vs S&P 0.78.
   Tested here: REGIME3 (boosted trees on raw candle shapes: memorised, AUC 0.5 after); WEEKEND A1 (kNN analogs of the opening path
   + prior two sessions, walk-forward, 4,128 sessions): AUC 0.50 for "trend day"; the 0.59 on "NoiseArea's day paid" equals the size
   of the move so far. Status: CLOSED. The published image skill is a ranking across thousands of stocks, not a day-type call on one.

7. REGIME DETECTION  (HMM / Markov switching, Hurst, variance ratio, efficiency ratio, choppiness)
   Mechanism: markets alternate between trending and mean-reverting states. Evidence: filtered (real-time) regime probabilities LAG;
   Christensen-Godsill-Turner (ES 1-min, one OOS year 2011, pre-cost Sharpe ~2 for spline side information, HMM variants mixed);
   blog/forum tests: 0 of 4 HMM variants beat buy-and-hold daily, intraday users did better ignoring the HMM; Safari-Schmidhuber 2025:
   trends exist only from hours to years, reversion below. Tested here: STATE (10-15), REGIME (trend efficiency, vol ratio), STRUCTURE
   (variance ratio, choppiness), REGIME_ML (learned), REGIME3 (later calls): all coin flips; the state-to-strategy map flips between
   decades. Status: CLOSED. Momentum/breakout earns and reversion loses in EVERY state tested; there is nothing to switch to.

8. BREADTH / MARKET INTERNALS  ($TICK, $ADD, $VOLD, 90% days, breadth thrusts)
   Mechanism: a trend day is broad participation; narrow participation reverts. Evidence: none out of sample for early-session
   readings -> rest of day (the search found no study, null or positive); session-level TICK extremes behave CONTRARIAN over 5-21
   days (thetrading.tools, 7,140 sessions, descriptive); 90% days and Zweig thrusts are multi-week signals; Chordia et al. 2002: daily
   market-wide imbalance has no next-day power. Practitioner rules ("ADD +/-150 in 15 min", "TICK never below -200") carry no sample.
   Data reality: Rithmic in NinjaTrader 8 delivers futures only ("F" on NinjaTrader's data-by-provider page) - no ^TICK/^ADD/^VOLD
   live or historical on this connection; internals need Kinetick / Continuum-CQG (NT Brokerage internals package ~$8/month, funded
   account) / IQFeed / IB; no free 1-minute history exists (Barchart Premier has 10 years, paid). NYSE TICK excludes Nasdaq megacaps.
   Tested here: not testable (no data). Status: OPEN but unfunded and without any evidence that it would work; lowest priority.

9. ORDER FLOW AT THE OPEN / TAPE READING  (imbalance, large prints, absorption, intensity)
   Mechanism: informed or forced flow at the open shows the day's direction/character. Evidence: Chordia et al. 2005 - imbalance
   predictability lasts under an hour; Cont et al. 2023 - OFI forecasts decay quickly; Adams et al. 2026 - flow effects decay within
   the hour; Takahashi 2025 - within seconds; Kang et al. 2022 - KOSPI OPTIONS flow in the first 10 minutes predicts the rest of the
   day (Korea, options, not transferable). No published early-order-flow trend-day predictor.
   Tested here: ORDERFLOW (NQ 2010-14 ticks before each entry: no feature |rho| > 0.05); WEEKEND A3 (261 sessions of NT ticks with
   the aggressor side, 09:45/10:00): every feature AUC 0.45-0.58 with a CI over 0.5. Status: CLOSED on owned data; the TapeRecorder
   keeps collecting, and a repeat after ~1,000 sessions (2029) would have the power for a 0.55 signal, which the DAYCALL bar says
   is not enough anyway.

10. RELATIVE VOLUME / VWAP STRUCTURE / VOLUME PROFILE
   Zarattini-Aziz ORB uses relative volume >= 1 as its filter; an independent MQL5 replication on five index CFDs 2015-2026: gross
   reproduced, NET ~zero. Tested here: RELVOL (dead: paying breakouts come at normal volume), VWAP trend (RULEMINE: statistically
   there, cost-dominated and negative since 2023), 242 volume-profile setups (MNQPropBook, dead). Status: CLOSED.

11. EVENT STRUCTURE  (FOMC, CPI/NFP, auctions, expiration, earnings, month-end)
   Tested here: ALPHA2, EVENTDAYS, EDGES3, MECH, LIT2026 - all dead intraday except the FOMC-eve overnight long (passes, not Bulenox-
   legal) and the third-Friday spike short (watch list, fails drop-5). Status: CLOSED except the watch list.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE PICTURE
  a. Patrick's own calls, time-stamped before each decision: patrick_calls.py scores them in one command against the labels the four
     regime studies used and against NoiseArea on the same sessions. If a labelled screenshot includes bars after the call, the model
     has learned hindsight; the AUC on calls made before 10:30 is the test. Bar: right on ~57% of days (AUC 0.60 meta / 0.65 eff).
  b. A gamma or breadth series with history would allow the only two mechanism-based reads left - both need paid data (SqueezeMetrics
     $720/mo, FlashAlpha $1,199/mo, internals via a CQG/Kinetick feed) and the free evidence says neither beats VIX / the bands.
  c. Nothing in the literature supports re-running price-bar day-type calls on more of the same bars.

THE SHAPE OF THE ANSWER
  "Read the structure, then apply the edge that fits" assumes two things: that the structure is readable early, and that a different
  edge exists for each structure. On Nasdaq futures over 16 years, the first is false for every reading tried (AUC 0.48-0.58, with the
  bar at 0.60-0.65), and the second is false for every catalog tried: momentum/breakout earns in every state, reversion loses in every
  state, including the one state (positive gamma) where the mechanism says reversion should pay. The live rules already contain the
  only structure read that works - wait until price has left the noise area - and pay a small loss when the read is wrong. That is
  why nothing beats them without giving up return.
