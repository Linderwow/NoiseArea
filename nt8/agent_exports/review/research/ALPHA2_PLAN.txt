PRE-REGISTERED TESTS: REMAINING WAYS TO RAISE RETURN OR CUT LOSSES   (written 2026-10-16 before any of these variants was run)

Already settled and NOT re-opened here: band multiplier, lookback, check cadence, trailing type, efficiency filter (684-variant
sweep); fixed stops and targets (STOPS_RESULTS.txt); adaptive re-tuning and equity-curve gating; volatility-targeted sizing;
the overnight Globex session as a separate strategy; other markets. Everything below is a different question.

Engine: a per-bar rewrite of the validated rules (same as stops.py) that must reproduce the validated engine's daily P&L to the
cent with every switch off, on both datasets, before any result is read. Data: NinjaTrader bars 2019-2026 (NT) and Databento
bars 2019-2025 (DB); discovery 2019-2022, validation 2023-end; 1 tick + $0.50 per side; warm-up 30 sessions excluded.

T1 FOMC days (economic prior: the 14:00 ET statement causes whipsaw inside the afternoon checks). FOMC decision dates 2019-2026
   from the Fed calendar (2020-03-03 and 2020-03-15 emergency cuts included; the latter is a Sunday and has no session).
   (a) skip FOMC days entirely; (b) on FOMC days go flat at the 13:30 check and take no entries until the 15:00 check;
   (c) report the baseline's P&L on FOMC days alone, both periods, both datasets.
   Also, same shape: quad-witching Fridays (third Friday of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec) skipped entirely.
T2 Hold the 16:00 position to the next session's 09:30 open instead of closing at 16:00 (momentum continuation overnight).
   (a) always; (b) only when the position shows a profit at 16:00. Exit at the next valid session's first bar open, 1 tick.
   Caveat recorded now: overnight holding changes margin (overnight margin is 10x intraday), may breach prop-firm rules, and
   carries gap risk the 7-year sample understates. Even a pass here is a candidate for a separate decision, not a rule change.
T3 Reversal replaced by flat: when the opposite band is crossed, go flat instead of flipping (the next check may still enter).
T4 Daily loss stop at X times the day's noise width in dollars, X in {1, 2, 3}: when realised + open P&L at a bar close is at or
   below -X * w * $2 (w = reference price x sigma at the first check), flatten at the next open and take no more entries that day.
T5 Parameter ensembles (reported, not a rule change): 1 micro each of VM {0.75, 1.0, 1.5} versus 3 micros of VM 1.0; 1 micro each
   of lookback {10, 14, 21} versus 3 of lookback 14. Compared on t-stat and max drawdown of the combined daily P&L.
T6 Entry window: first check in {10:00 (base), 10:30, 11:00} x last entry check in {15:30 (base), 14:30, 13:30}, exits unchanged.
   The adaptive experiment logged "entries at 14:00 lose in sample" as an observation; this is the pre-registered way to look.

Decision rule, set now: a variant is adopted only if it beats the baseline on BOTH net and daily t-stat in discovery AND validation
on BOTH datasets, with a validation t gain of at least 0.3 on both, and (for T4 and T6, which are grids) its one-step neighbours
also beat the baseline on validation net on both datasets. T1 additionally requires that the baseline loses money on the affected
days in both periods on both datasets (the mechanism must be visible, not just the aggregate). T2 is reported only. T5 is reported
only. Anything that fails is logged and the rules stay as they are.
