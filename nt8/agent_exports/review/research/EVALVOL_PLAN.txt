PRE-REGISTERED: VOLATILITY-SCALED MICROS IN THE BULENOX EVALUATION   (written 2026-10-22 before any number below was computed)

Why: Sashkin, 2026-10-22: "make it more profitable ... utilize everything we can". The evaluation's micros are the biggest lever
found (BIGLEVER: 50K Qualification at 6 micros + $78 resets). At a fixed 6 micros Bulenox's $1,100 daily loss limit is touched on
about 1 session in 5, mostly on volatile days. Volatility-scaled sizing was tested for the STRATEGY in 2026-10 (SIZING_PLAN:
1-3 contracts, judged on t-stat - failed); this tests it for a different objective - passing a fixed-drawdown, daily-limited
evaluation inside 30 days - which that test did not measure. Nothing in NoiseArea's rules changes.

SIZE RULE (known before the open): s_t = the strategy's own noise-area sigma at the 16:00 slot (mean |close/open - 1| over the
previous 14 sessions); s_ref = median of s_t over NQ 2010-07 .. 2019-05 (fixed, never re-estimated).
 E1  micros_t = clip(round(6 x s_ref / s_t), 2, 10)        (scales both ways around 6)
 E2  micros_t = clip(round(6 x s_ref / s_t), 2, 6)         (scales down only, never above 6)
 base: 6 micros every day (BIGLEVER's choice for the 50K evaluation)

MODEL: exactly biglever.py's evaluation (50K Qualification, Option 2: $3,000 target, $2,500 end-of-day drawdown trailing the closing
high, intraday breaches from each session's worst point, $1,100 daily loss limit, $78 reset with >= 5 sessions left, 21 sessions,
fee $175); daily P&L = micros_t x the 1-micro series (intraday_worst.csv). (a) 20,000 bootstrap windows (21-session blocks, seed
20261022, the same windows for every rule); (b) every real consecutive window by era as in biglever_eras.py: NQ 2010-19, MNQ
2019-26, last 24 months, last 12 months.

DECISION: a rule is BETTER only if (1) its expected fees per funded account on (a) are at least 10% below base's, AND (2) its pass
rate per try is not below base's in the last 24 months AND in the last 12 months of (b), AND (3) it is not below base's on both long
eras of (b). Two rules, so (1) uses 10% rather than any significance claim; the eras guard against a lucky fit. A BETTER rule needs
a NinjaScript option (contracts from the indicator's sigma), an offline compile and an Analyzer check before Sashkin uses it;
otherwise the evaluation stays at a fixed 6 micros.
Reported, not gated: average micros, share of days at the floor / cap, daily-loss-limit touches per window.
