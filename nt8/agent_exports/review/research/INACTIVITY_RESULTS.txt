AUDIT (inactivity_check.py, strategy-validation protocol)
- sample count: fallback sessions with V = 1 equal sum(run - K + 1) over dry runs >= K (362). The audit's first formula was wrong
  (sum(run - K) = 174, FAIL): the session that ends a dry run is a fallback session too - it is where the trade happens. Fixed.
- synthetic paths: closure at exactly D dry sessions (5 and 6), the fallback switches on at dry >= K and stays on while untraded.
- no lookahead: a session's fallback flag never changes when later sessions are shuffled.
- eras: NQ 2010-2019 100 sessions +$15 (t +0.42); MNQ 2019-2026 98 sessions +$0.2 (t +0.01). Per year: signs mixed, no dominant year.
- drop the 5 best fallback sessions: -$22.8 (t -1.06) - not significantly negative; the fallback is noise around zero, not a drag.
- control: V = 0 on EVERY session minus the base: -$7.0/session (t -1.40); V = 0 alone $12.5 (t +1.84) vs base $19.5 (t +4.33) -
  so V = 0 is the worse rule in general and is used only where the base rule is silent.
- second instrument MES 2019-06 .. 2026-05 (Databento 5-min): 24 dry runs >= 5 without, 0 with the fallback; fallback sessions 92,
  -3.3 bps gross (t -0.62).
- known limit: bootstrap blocks (21 sessions) cut or join dry runs; equal for every variant; the historical walk (no blocks) agrees.
