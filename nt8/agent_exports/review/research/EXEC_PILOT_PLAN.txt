2. SIGNALS AND SIDES. The trades of research\s1_reference_trades_ntbars_v3.csv (the parity reference the live strategy matches) in
 the sessions covered: no signals are recomputed. Each trade gives two sides (1 micro each; a reversal = an exit side and an entry
 side at the same moment, same direction). Decision time T and backtest reference price R per side:
   signal side (fill bar stamped 10:05 ... 15:35): T = fill-bar stamp - 5 min; backtest fill = next bar's open, so R = the first
   trade at or after T on the tape (bar open);
   flat side (exit stamped 16:00, or 12:55 / 13:10 on partial sessions): T = that stamp; backtest fill = that bar's close, so R = the
   last trade before T.
 The backtest charges R + 1 tick adverse on every side. All costs below are in ticks per side, positive = worse than R, measured on
 the file's own prices (so a back-adjusted reference price never enters a cost).
 Roll rule (fixed now): the live strategy rolls on the roll Thursday (8 days before expiry) while the files hold the expiring
 contract until expiry, so sessions from the roll Thursday to expiry (2025-12-11..12-19, 2026-03-12..03-20) are on a contract the
 strategy would not trade. They are reported separately and excluded from the primary figures. Each session's RTH volume in the
 file is printed so the thinning can be seen.
 Consequence known before running: Part A has 10 reference trades in the file (Dec 5-19; the two Dec-31 trades are past the file's
 end), and 9 of them fall in the roll window - only Dec 5 (1 trade, 2 sides) is on the front contract. Part A is therefore reported
 in full (20 sides, flagged) but cannot carry a verdict. Part B: 62 trades outside the March roll window (3 more inside it).

3. MARKET-ORDER COST (current execution). NT8 closes a bar when the first tick after its end arrives; the order goes out then.
 Latency assumed: order arrives at t1 = t0 + 250 ms, t0 = the first trade at or after T (sensitivity 0 ms and 1,000 ms, Part B).
  M1s (both parts) = the adverse extreme of the first 1-second bar at or after T (buy: its high, sell: its low) - the brief's measure.
      In Part B the 1-second bars are built from the tape itself (ts_event floored to the second), so M1s is computed identically.
  Mtouch (Part B, primary) = the touch at t1 rebuilt from the last trade with a known side at or before t1: buyer at p -> ask p,
      bid p - 1 tick; seller at p -> bid p, ask p + 1 tick (1-tick spread assumed). Buy fills at the ask, sell at the bid.
  Mnext (Part B, check on the spread assumption) = what the next same-side aggressor paid: first 'B' trade (buy) / 'A' trade (sell)
      at or after t1.
 Reported: mean, median and spread of cost per side with a 95% bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples of decision events - reversal legs
 move together - seed 20261019), by side type (entry / signal exit / flat), and for Part B Mtouch at 0 / 250 / 1,000 ms.
 Verdict on the 1-tick assumption (Part B Mtouch at 250 ms): ADEQUATE if mean <= 1.0, BORDERLINE if 1.0-1.5, BREACH if > 1.5 tick
 (the live gate's threshold) - a breach would be reported as a finding for the cost model; nothing in the code changes either way.

4. PASSIVE ALTERNATIVES (6 variants, fixed now, no others). Decision price C = the last trade before T (= the decision bar's close).
 Limit L = C - s*k ticks (s = +1 buy, -1 sell), k in {0, 1}; sent at the same moment as the market order (t1); held until T + H,
 H in {10, 30, 60} s; if unfilled, cancelled and replaced by a market order at T + H (cost measured as in section 3 at that moment,
 so every side still gets filled: the cost of a miss is a price difference, never a lost trade).
 Fill rules (a limit's queue position is unknown without depth data, so two bounds):
  CONSERVATIVE (primary): Part B - fills at once at the touch if L is marketable at t1 (buy: L >= ask(t1)); otherwise fills at L only
      if a trade prints at least 1 tick through L (buy: price <= L - 1 tick) after t1 and before T + H. Part A - fills at L only if a
      1-second bar after the first one at/after T and before T + H trades 1 tick through L (no marketability test possible).
  OPTIMISTIC (bound): fills at L on any print at L or better inside the window (Part A: from the first 1-second bar on).
 Reported per variant and part: fill rate, mean cost per side, mean SAVING vs the market order (market cost - passive cost; positive =
 cheaper) with 95% CI, mean saving on filled sides, mean extra cost on missed sides (the cost of misses), worst single side, and the
 saving on the entry sides of the trades that ran furthest (top 20% of the pilot's trades by final points) versus the rest - a
 momentum entry that waits tends to be filled on the trades that come back and missed on the ones that run.
 Part B is computed twice - with the tape rules above and with the Part-A 1-second rules on the 1-second bars built from the same
 tape - which is the calibration of the 1-second method.

5. DECISION RULES (fixed now)
 PROMISING variant: conservative mean saving >= +0.5 tick/side on Part B (about $110/yr per micro at 452 sides a year - the size
   that would justify an order-handling code change and its risk), and not negative on Part A's front-contract sides.
 1-SECOND BARS ADEQUATE (Part B calibration): (i) the 1-second market cost M1s is within 0.25 tick/side of Mtouch on average, and
   (ii) for the best Part-B variant the 1-second conservative saving has the same sign as the tape saving and is within 0.5 tick/side.
 PURCHASE ($20.86, one year of 1-second cash-session bars):
   GO if a variant is PROMISING and 1-second bars are ADEQUATE (the year then tests that one variant, pre-registered first);
   GO if the market-order cost is above 1.25 tick/side on Part B (cost model in doubt) and 1-second bars pass (i);
   otherwise NO-GO. If a variant is promising but 1-second bars are not adequate, NO-GO on that purchase (it cannot answer the
   question); say what data would.
 Power check (reported): the CI half-width a year (~452 sides, ~226 trades) would give, from Part B's per-event spread of the best
   variant's saving. A half-width above 0.5 tick/side means a year of data could not settle a 0.5-tick question either.
 SIM101 EXECUTION TRIAL: recommended only if the purchase is GO on the PROMISING branch, and then not on Sim101 while its 20-session
   gate runs (it would change the execution being gated) and only with NinjaTrader's simulator fill model taken into account
   (it fills a resting limit on touch, i.e. the optimistic bound). Otherwise NO.
 Multiple testing: 6 variants x 2 fill bounds on one small sample; the best variant's saving is biased upward and is quoted as such.

6. CHECKS BEFORE REPORTING (triple-check)
 (1) R from the tape equals the open of the Databento 1-minute bar starting at T (signal sides) / the close of the 1-minute bar
     ending at T (flat sides) from the 1-minute files - same contract (both MNQ.c.0) - on every side.
 (2) R minus the reference file's price: one constant offset per contract period (back-adjustment) and per-side residuals within
     +-1 tick on most sides - confirms the time alignment with NinjaTrader's bars.
 (3) Three sides printed with their raw trades / 1-second bars and every estimator worked by hand.
 (4) Cost signs checked on a synthetic case (a buy at a higher price must cost a positive number of ticks).
