BOTTOM LINE: NOT PROMISING at either delta - keep the 10:00 decision on the bar close.
 - Sending the order 1 s early does look cheaper on the trade tape: +3.34 ticks/side saved at 75 ms, and +2.29 after the quote
   adjustment. But the early decision differs from the real one on 1.6% of days. In the NinjaTrader tick year those changes cost
   $205 while the saving was worth $105. Over seven years, the missed entries alone cost $73-145 a year, against about $77-112 a
   year saved.
 - At 250 ms early, NinjaTrader's recorded quotes wipe out the saving (-0.51 tick after the A1 adjustment). Even before that
   adjustment, the 2023-2026 CI crosses zero.
 - The missed entries are not random. They are days when the price pushed into the band in the last second before 10:00, which is
   the momentum the strategy trades. Missing them at 10:00 means entering later at a worse price.
 - SIDE FINDING (affects the cost model, not this verdict): MNQ spreads around 10:00 are wider than the 1-tick touch model assumes.
   This test uses that model and so does LIMIT_RESULTS. NinjaTrader's quotes show a median of 2-3 ticks in 2025-26, and 14-20 ticks
   at the 90th percentile in the seconds around 10:00:00. The Databento trades agree. So the +2.6 ticks/side cost of the real 10:00
   entries is probably a floor for 2025-26. Only live fill logging (INSTITUTIONAL_OPS item 6) can measure the true number.
