CODED RULES (as taught; where a video leaves a choice open, the first listed value is the as-taught default, the others are the
variants the walk-forward may pick)
 Y1 15-min ORB (V1): OR = 09:30-09:44 high/low. Buy stop OR high + 2 ticks, sell stop OR low - 2 ticks, live 09:45-12:00. Filter at the
    minute before the fill: session VWAP (from 09:30) rising over the last 15 minutes for a long (falling for a short) and price on
    that side of VWAP. Stop: other side of the OR -/+ 2 ticks. Target: OR high + OR height - 2 ticks (mirror). When price reaches half
    an OR height beyond the level, stop to entry + 1 tick. Up to 2 entries per direction, one position at a time; flat 15:59.
    Variants: VWAP filter off; target 2 x OR height.
 Y2 EMA-stack + counter-trend FVG inversion (V2), 5-minute chart: bias at 09:30 from 50-period EMAs of 15-, 30- and 60-minute closes
    (stacked 15 > 30 > 60 with the last 5-minute close above all three and each EMA rising = long bias; mirror = short; else no trade).
    The latest bearish 5-minute FVG (low[i-2] > high[i]) formed 08:00 .. signal and not yet closed above; the first 5-minute close
    above its top between 09:35 and 10:00 = long at the next open (mirror). Stop: lowest low since that FVG formed - 1 tick.
    Target 2R. One trade a day. Variants: target 3R; stop = low of the breaking candle.
 Y3 VWAP 2-sigma bounce (V5), 5-minute chart, 09:45-15:00: a bar whose low is at or below VWAP - 2 sigma (volume-weighted) and closes
    back above it, with RSI(14) of 5-minute closes rising -> long at the next open, stop that bar's low - 1 tick, target VWAP at the
    signal (mirror at +2 sigma). Up to 2 a day, one at a time, flat 15:59. Variants: no RSI condition; 3-sigma stop instead of the low.
 Y4 15-min ORB, close then retest (V8): OR = 09:30-09:44. The first 5-minute close above the OR high (below the low) from 09:50 on sets a
    limit at the OR high (low), live until 12:00 or until price reaches the target first. Stop: OR midpoint. Target 1R. One trade a day.
    Variant: target 2R.
 Y5 Opening fair price (V9), 1-minute chart: open price = 09:30 open; opening candle = the 09:30 minute. Continuation, 09:31-09:40: a
    displacement candle (body larger than the previous bar's whole range, wicks at most 25% of its range) in the opening candle's
    direction -> enter at the next open. Reversion, 09:40-11:00: when price is at least the stop distance away from the open price, a
    displacement candle back toward it -> enter at the next open. Stop 25 points, target 38 points (at the 2026 price level; scaled to
    the day's price as a share of it). At most 4 trades a day, one at a time. Variants: 5-minute opening candle; target = the open
    price for reversion trades.
 Y6 Open sweep and reclaim (V12, model 1), 1-minute chart, 09:30-09:45: bias long if the prior RTH close is above its 20-day average
    close (short if below). Long: a minute trades below the overnight low (18:00-09:29), then a minute closes above the high of the
    minute that made that low -> long at the next open; stop = that low - 1 tick; target 2R; stop to break-even at +1R. Mirror for
    shorts. One a day. Variants: target the overnight high; no bias filter.
 Y7 9/21 EMA pullback (V13), 1-hour chart, round the clock: while EMA9 > EMA21, an hour whose low touches EMA21 and closes above EMA9 ->
    long at the next hour's open; stop = lowest low of the last 3 hours - 1 tick; target 2R (mirror). One position at a time; closed
    before any contract change. Variants: 3R; exit when EMA9 crosses back below EMA21 (no target).
 Y8 Asia range London sweep (V15): Asia range = 20:00-01:29 high/low; the day trades only if the range is under 2% and both its high and
    its low were made between 20:30 and 00:30 (sideways, not trending). 02:00-05:00: a minute trades through the range and closes back
    inside -> reversal at the next open; stop beyond that minute's extreme + 1 tick; target 2R; out by 07:59. One a day.
    Variants: target the other side of the range; target 1.5R.
