BOTTOM LINE
 The biggest lever left is not a trading rule: it is HOW MANY MICROS NoiseArea trades DURING THE BULENOX EVALUATION. At 1 micro the
 evaluation is the bottleneck (25K passes 18% of 30-day tries, 50K 2.4%). Sized up - where only the fee is at risk - the same
 strategy passes far more often: 50K at 6-8 micros with the $78 reset 60-68% per try (fees per funded account $7,277 -> $366-388),
 25K at 3-4 micros 58-65% ($806 -> ~$300). Then trade the funded Master at 1 micro (the brief's sizing gate) and the value of one
 account slot rises from about $1,000 to about $2,400 over two years (50K) - x2.4; with 4 funded micros x3.1, but then only 18% of
 Masters are still open after a year. Fast Track and Momentum accounts are NOT better for NoiseArea (smaller drawdowns, stricter
 payout rules). Nothing in NoiseArea's rules changes; this is a business decision for Sashkin and it costs money (fees), so nothing
 is bought or switched here.

THE CORRECTION THAT MATTERED (amendment 1, declared after the first run)
 The first run used daily closes only and chose 10 micros everywhere (e.g. 25K evaluation 80% per try). Bulenox stops accounts
 intraday: the daily loss limit counts open P&L and the drawdown is breached by the live account value. With each session's
 intraday worst point (intraday_worst.py; 1 day in 10 dips below -$301 per micro, 1 in 100 below -$764) the same 25K evaluation at
 10 micros passes 31%, and its best size falls to 3-4 micros. Every figure below is the intraday model. Still optimistic: the stop
 is assumed to fill exactly at the limit; the 16-year history includes the strategy's best eras; live results so far are 5 sessions.
