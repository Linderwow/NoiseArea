METALABEL RESULTS (2026-10-24; plan METALABEL_PLAN.txt written before the run; metalabel.py -> metalabel_output.txt)

BOTTOM LINE - REJECTED. A model trained on NoiseArea's own past entries (15 features known at the entry: breakout strength, time,
volatility regime, gap, VWAP distance, relative volume, VIX and its term structure, the strategy's own recent run, ...) has a
little skill - out-of-sample AUC 0.555 (logistic) / 0.559 (boosted stumps), 0.5 = none - but skipping the entries it scores worst
makes the strategy WORSE on every version: return per drawdown (MAR) 1.30 for the live rules out of sample (2016-01 .. 2026-10-23)
vs 0.88-0.96 filtered; drawdowns deeper ($5,700-6,500 vs $4,800 per micro); no gate passes (bootstrap P 0.44-0.78 against a 0.0125
bar). Why: only 37% of NoiseArea's trades win and the money is in a few large trend-day winners; a filter that is right a little
more often than chance still skips some of those, and they are worth far more than the small losers it avoids.

Audit: the simulator with no filter reproduces nav_lab.fam_V exactly on NQ and MNQ (daily points and trades). 3,607 entries
2010-07 .. 2026-10 (2,405 out of sample), refit every January, anchored.
