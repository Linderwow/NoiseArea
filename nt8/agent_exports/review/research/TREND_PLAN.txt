PRE-REGISTERED TEST: A MULTI-DAY TREND-FOLLOWING SLEEVE ON NASDAQ-100 FUTURES BESIDE NOISEAREA
(written 2026-10-19, before any trend number was computed and before the FRED series was downloaded)

Question: does a separate, documented multi-day trend model on the same market (Nasdaq-100 futures), run BESIDE the unchanged
NoiseArea strategy, add risk-adjusted profit to the combined portfolio - more than simply holding the index would? NoiseArea is
not changed in any way. Research only: no NinjaScript, nothing compiled, nothing live touched.

Why this and not another NoiseArea rule: every rule change failed (spec section 9). NoiseArea's weakness is a lumpy payoff (best 1%
of days = 77% of 16 years' profit, flat years 2013-2017 and 2019, worst drawdown 14% of notional = $7,900 per micro in 2010-11).
A slow trend model earns from multi-day/multi-month drift, a different horizon from NoiseArea's intraday breakouts, so its daily
P&L may be weakly correlated with NoiseArea's and smooth the combination.

Literature (the family is taken from it, nothing invented): Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen (2012) time-series momentum - sign of the
past 12-month excess return, also 1/3/6 months; Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen (2017) equal-weighted 1/3/12-month trend; Brock, Lakonishok &
LeBaron (1992) and Faber (2007) price versus its 50/150/200-day (10-month) moving average. Known caveat, recorded now: on ONE equity
index with a strong upward drift a trend rule mostly cuts drawdowns rather than raising the Sharpe ratio, and any long-biased rule
looks good in a rising Nasdaq - hence benchmark (a) below.

DAILY ACCOUNTING (one row per session date d; a session's decision is taken at its 16:00 ET close)
  C_d = close of the 5-minute bar stamped 16:00 ET (back-adjusted; the futures' settlement time).  F_d = open of the next bar
  (stamped 16:05) = fill price of any position change decided at C_d (NoiseArea's own convention: decide at a bar close, fill at
  the next bar's open); F_d = C_d if that bar is missing. R_d = RAW (unadjusted) 16:00 close of the front contract from the
  Databento files; the NT dataset uses the Databento raw close of the same date (a unit conversion only; NT's own prices drive
  every signal and every point of P&L).
  Partial sessions (no 16:00 bar: 13:00/13:15 closes): no decision; the position is carried and marked at the session's last bar.
  Excess return of 1 unit long: x_d = (C_d - C_{d-1}) / R_{d-1}  (adjusted change = a rolled long position's points, over notional).
  Position p_d (units of 1 contract) decided at C_d, filled at F_d. Daily return on notional:
    ret_d = p_{d-1} x_d + (p_d - p_{d-1}) (C_d - F_d)/R_d - |p_d - p_{d-1}| c_d - roll_d |p_{d-1}| 2 c_d
  roll_d = 1 on a contract-switch date (Databento instrument change; the NT dataset is charged on the same dates).
  Costs per side, c_d as a fraction of notional:
    (a) ACTUAL: 1 tick + commission of the contract traded: NQ (0.25 x $20 + $2.00)/($20 R_d); MNQ (0.25 x $2 + $0.50)/($2 R_d).
    (b) TODAY'S RELATIVE COST: 0.5 points / P_ref on every day (what 1 tick + $0.50 is on MNQ in 2026), as LONGHIST_PLAN.txt.
  The VERDICT uses (b) for both legs (NoiseArea's section-7 expectations are on (b); the question is about trading today); (a) is
  reported beside it. Dollars: today's $ per micro = return x $2 x P_ref, P_ref fixed at 27,875 (LONGHIST: mean 2026 MNQ 09:30
  open) so every figure is comparable with spec section 7 (notional $55,750 per micro).

SIGNAL INDEX AND THE FAMILY (16 variants, no others)
  I = cumulative product of (1 + x_d): the excess-return index of the rolled future. Discovery: FRED cash-index returns before
  2010-06-07, NQ after. Validation: NQ returns up to 2019-05-03, the junction day 2019-05-06 = Databento MNQ raw close vs NQ raw
  close of 2019-05-03 (same June-2019 contract), MNQ after (each dataset its own). Signals are computed at C_d from I up to and
  including d (no later data).
  TSMOM-L : s = sign(I_d / I_{d-L} - 1),            L = 21, 63, 126, 252 sessions (about 1, 3, 6, 12 months)
  SMA-N   : s = sign(I_d - mean(I_{d-N+1} .. I_d)), N = 50, 100, 200 sessions
  Modes: LONG/FLAT p = 1 if s > 0 else 0;  LONG/SHORT p = s.   7 signals x 2 modes = 14.
  Ensembles: EW-LF = mean of the 7 long/flat positions, EW-LS = mean of the 7 long/short positions (fractional units; 7 micros per
  unit in practice).  = 16 variants.  Benchmark B&H: p = 1 always (pays the roll costs).
  Not tested (would widen the family): volatility-scaled positions, other look-backs, other markets, crossovers of two averages.

NOISEAREA LEG
  research_engine.fam_A with the validated defaults, run on NQ 5-minute (discovery), MNQ Databento 5-minute (DB) and NinjaTrader
  bars (NT); converted to return on notional with the raw 09:30 open (raw_open930.csv) and to cost basis (b) by linearity, exactly as
  longhist.py. 30-session warm-up excluded as everywhere.

PERIODS (the combined comparison always uses the same days for every series)
  Discovery  = NQ, first NoiseArea day after warm-up (2010-07-21) .. 2018-12-31.
  Validation = MNQ, first NoiseArea day after warm-up (about 2019-06-17) .. last day of each dataset (DB and NT separately).
  16-year joined series = NQ 2010-07-21..2019-05-03 + MNQ validation (both versions), as LONGHIST.

METRICS (every series, today's $ per unit): annualised Sharpe (daily mean/sd x sqrt 252), daily t-stat, net per year, max
  drawdown (+ dates), longest time under water (sessions), worst rolling 12 months, CUSHION PER UNIT OF EXPECTED PROFIT =
  max drawdown / mean annual profit (the house cushion is 1.5 x worst drawdown, so the 1.5 cancels in any comparison).
  Correlation NoiseArea vs trend: daily, monthly, and on the 5% worst days of each leg (tail co-movement).
  Bootstrap (10,000 synthetic 250-day years): P(losing year), 99th-percentile within-year drawdown / median year. Informational.

BENCHMARKS (written now)
  (a) Buy-and-hold at matched volatility: B&H scaled to the trend variant's daily sd on the same days. Sharpe is scale-free, so
      "beats B&H risk-adjusted" = Sharpe(trend) > Sharpe(B&H) on the same days; matched-vol net and drawdown and the regression alpha
      of trend on B&H (annualised, OLS t) are reported. Applied ALSO at portfolio level: NoiseArea + trend must beat NoiseArea +
      B&H, each leg sized by the same rule - otherwise the trend model adds nothing that plain index exposure would not.
  (b) Combined portfolio versus NoiseArea alone: combined = NoiseArea (1 micro) + w x trend, w = sd(NoiseArea)/sd(trend) on the
      DISCOVERY days (equal risk), fixed and never re-estimated; the B&H leg gets its own w_BH by the same rule. Reported beside it
      (never selected): w = 1 (one micro of each), the hindsight w from validation, Sharpe for w = 0, 0.1 .. 1.0, and the nearest
      whole-micro ratio (e.g. 3 NoiseArea : 1 trend).

SELECTION (on discovery only, one candidate)
  1. Eligible = discovery net > 0 AND discovery Sharpe > B&H's discovery Sharpe (cost basis (b)).
  2. Candidate = the eligible variant with the highest discovery Sharpe of NoiseArea + w x trend.
  3. No eligible variant -> FAIL at discovery; all 16 are still reported on validation (reported, never selected).

ADOPTION BAR (ALL must hold, cost basis (b); on BOTH validation datasets DB and NT unless stated)
  V1 Out of sample: candidate net > 0 AND Sharpe > B&H Sharpe (same days).
  V2 Portfolio: Sharpe(NoiseArea + w trend) > Sharpe(NoiseArea alone) AND > Sharpe(NoiseArea + w_BH B&H).
  V3 Cushion: cushion per unit of expected profit of NoiseArea + w trend <= NoiseArea alone's, on both validation datasets AND on
     both 16-year joined series.
  V4 Plateau: each neighbour - same signal and mode, one look-back step either side (TSMOM 21-63-126-252, SMA 50-100-200); for an
     ensemble, each of its 7 leave-one-out ensembles - also has validation net > 0, Sharpe > B&H, and improves the combined Sharpe
     over NoiseArea alone, on both validation datasets. A lone good look-back is noise (684-variant and stops lessons).
  Pass -> a RESEARCH result only: it would be a candidate for a separate decision by Aleksandr (overnight holding, account rules,
  margin), then its own NinjaScript, audit, parity and Sim run. Fail -> logged in spec section 9; nothing changes either way.

REPORTED, NEVER SELECTED: cost basis (a); a one-day execution delay (signal at C_d, filled at C_{d+1}); costs x 3; all 16 variants
  on validation; year-by-year table of NoiseArea, candidate, B&H and the combination; block-bootstrap p-value of the Sharpe
  difference candidate - B&H (63-session blocks).
