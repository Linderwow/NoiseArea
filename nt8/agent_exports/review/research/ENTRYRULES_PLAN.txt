ENTRYRULES - PRE-REGISTERED ONE-AT-A-TIME TEST OF THE RULE PARAMETERS NEVER ISOLATED  (written 2026-10-23 12:18 ET, before any run)

Why: the user asked "why not check more frequently than every half hour?" and "go over all the rules and retest if needed to improve
the entries". Already tested and NOT adopted (not re-run here): NAVAR V1-V9 (long-only, half-size shorts, 90-day lookback, fast
execution, exit persistence gate, lunch pause, EMA confirmation, shock sizing, no gap adjustment), DAYCAP C1-C4 (trade caps, stop
after losses, no re-entry), STOPS grid (stops / targets: a ridge, not a plateau), ALPHA2 (7 structural ideas), EARLYFIRE (order
before 10:00:00), LIMIT (limit entries lose 5.7 ticks a side), VARIANTS / regime grid (frequency, band and exit only in COMBINATION
with other changes; DEAD on validation). Never tested alone with every other rule at the published base case: the decision
frequency, the first decision time, the band width and the exit rule. That is this test.

BASE = the live rules exactly (nav_lab.fam_V defaults: VM 1.0, lookback 14, decisions at 10:00, 10:30 .. 15:30 on the 5-minute
bar close, fill at the next bar's open, exit when the close crosses back past max(upper band, VWAP) / min(lower band, VWAP), flat
at 16:00, gap-adjusted band base).

VARIANTS (K = 10; each changes ONE rule, everything else = base)
 F5    decisions every 5 minutes 10:00 .. 15:30 (every bar)            F10  every 10 minutes 10:00 .. 15:30
 F15   every 15 minutes 10:00 .. 15:30                                 F60  every 60 minutes 10:00 .. 15:00
 S0945 base schedule plus one extra decision at 09:45                  S1030 first decision 10:30 (no 10:00 decision)
 B080  band multiplier VM 0.8 (entries trigger earlier)                B120 VM 1.2 (entries need a bigger move)
 XBAND exit when the close crosses back inside the BAND only (ignore VWAP)
 XVWAP exit when the close crosses back past VWAP only (ignore the band)

PAYER / MECHANISM: the strategy's payer is the intraday momentum of index futures documented by Zarattini / Aziz / Barbon (2024):
late, size-constrained flows (hedgers, rebalancers, leveraged ETFs) that keep pushing in the direction of an established move.
Checking more often or with a narrower band enters earlier in a move that continues but also takes more false breakouts in noise
and pays more costs; the paper's half-hour grid and VM 1 balance that. Each variant asks whether the balance point is wrong.

DATA AND COSTS (identical to NAVAR_PLAN.txt): 16-year joined series NQ 2010-2019 + MNQ NinjaTrader bars 2019-05 .. 2026-10
(primary), MNQ Databento bars 2019-2026 (second source), costs 1 tick + $0.50 a side per micro (size-weighted), stress 2 ticks;
fresh markets ES 2010-2019, MES / MYM / M2K 2019-2026 (gross). The fresh markets are the held-out evidence.

GATES (identical to NAVAR, K = 10, so the bootstrap bar is 0.05 / 10 = 0.005). A variant is RECOMMENDED only if ALL hold:
 D1 paired month-block bootstrap (10,000): P(MAR_V <= MAR_base) < 0.005 and Sharpe_V >= Sharpe_base - 0.05
 D2 MAR_V > MAR_base on NQ 2010-2019, on MNQ NinjaTrader 2019-2026 and on MNQ Databento 2019-2026
 D3 fresh markets: gross Sharpe_V > base on ES and on at least 2 of MES / MYM / M2K
 D5 D1 and D2 still hold at 2 ticks a side
 D6 Bulenox 50K Master model: 2-year payouts and accounts open after 1 year not worse than base
Plus the strategy-validation protocol (reported for every variant, a recommended variant must pass them too):
 Z  zero-cost diagnostic: the variant's gain must not exist only at zero cost / disappear at real cost
 R5 drop the 5 best days of the variant: still better MAR than base (5 best days dropped there too)
 Y  per-year table: no single year may carry the whole improvement (sum of yearly gains minus the best year > 0)
 P  plateau (F and B variants): the neighbouring setting in the same direction must not be worse than base (F5/F10/F15 are each
    other's neighbours; B080 <-> VM 0.9, B120 <-> VM 1.1, run only if B080 / B120 pass everything else)

DECISION: a RECOMMENDED variant goes to a pre-registered Sim101 forward test (paper ledger beside the live rules), never straight
into the live strategy. If none passes: the rules stay exactly as they are, and the answer to "why only every half hour" is the
data. Trial count: these 10 (session total of rule variants tested on this strategy since 2026-10-15: VARIANTS 432 family-A cells
+ NAVAR 9 + DAYCAP 4 + STOPS grid + ALPHA2 7 + EARLYFIRE 2 + LIMIT + these 10); the D1 bar already corrects for the 10 here, and the
fresh-market gate D3 is the out-of-sample protection against the wider search.
