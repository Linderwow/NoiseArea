  At today's price level the same returns are about twice as large in dollars, so the gate's band (-$598 .. +$1,114) is breached by
  chance far more often than the 10% it was built for:
     rolling 20-session P&L below -$598:  15.1% of days (16 years), 10.7% (2019-2026)
     above +$1,114:                        20.9% (16 years), 21.3% (2019-2026)

  Method note (2026-10-20): MONITOR_PLAN.txt said "from the same bootstrap"; the rates above are rolling historical windows. The
  month-block bootstrap (2,000 paths x 120 months) gives below -$598 on 17.2% (16 years) / 13.1% (2019-05-06 .. 2026-10-14) of
  days, above +$1,114 on 22.5% / 23.6%; 5th / 95th percentile -$1,248 / +$2,254 and -$1,077 / +$2,276. Same conclusion. "Today's
  price" is p_ref = $27,875, the mean 2026 09:30 open (notional $55,751 per micro), not the last price.
  Consequences: (i) the Section 8 "live vs backtest" gate would fail an intact strategy about a third of the time (both tails);

  one day in seven to ten by chance. Restating the band in today's dollars (or as a share of notional) before the 20th session is a
  units fix, not a result-driven change, but it is a gate, so it is Sashkin's call.
