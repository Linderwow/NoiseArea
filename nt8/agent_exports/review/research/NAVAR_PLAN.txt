VARIANTS (k = 9)
 V1 Long-only: short entries suppressed. ALREADY SEEN on NQ+MNQ (MNQ_PROGRAM_RESULTS observation; 16-year $3,159/yr, Sharpe 1.09,
    maxDD $2,900 vs $4,739 / 1.08 / $7,906), so its 16-year gates are reported but the deciding evidence is D3 (fresh markets) and
    the Databento source. Sources: Quantitativo ES/NQ (longs +6 bp vs shorts +3 bp a trade), Concretum FAQ Q5/Q7/Q21 (shorts are the
    crisis leg - against).
 V2 Long/short 2:1: shorts at half size (Concretum FAQ; scout 3). At 1 micro not tradable; Bulenox model at 2 micros (long 2, short 1).
 V3 90-day sigma lookback (Concretum FAQ Q6: SPY Sharpe 1.50 / DD 13% vs 1.35 / 23% at 14 days). Plateau: 60 and 120 days reported
    and required (D4).
 V4 Fast-alpha execution overlay (Zarattini & Pagani 2026, read in full): a change of position decided at a check is not filled at
    the next bar; a BUY (long entry, short exit, short->long) waits for the first later 5-minute bar that closes below its open, a
    SELL waits for the first bar that closes above its open, and fills at the following bar's open. A new check replaces a pending
    order (cancelled if the new decision equals the current position). The session-end flat is unchanged.
 V5 Exit persistence gate (BlackSwan Quants / francesco-nicolo replication, SPY): while in a position, the exit condition (long:
    close < max(upper band, VWAP); short: close > min(lower band, VWAP)) is checked on every 5-minute bar from the entry onward; the
    position is closed at the next bar's open only after 4 consecutive bars in violation (20 minutes); the counter resets on any bar
    not in violation. Entries (from flat) stay on the 30-minute checks; no reversal without first going flat through the gate.
 V6 Lunch pause (Concretum FAQ Q18): no entries from flat at the 12:00, 12:30 and 13:00 checks; open positions are managed as usual.
 V7 EMA confirmation (BlackSwan S2): a long entry also needs close > EMA(20) of 5-minute RTH closes (carried across sessions), a
    short entry close < EMA(20). Exits unchanged.
 V8 Intraday shock control (Chris-ZZX, SPY): RV = sum of squared 5-minute log returns since 09:30; H = median RV at the same slot over
    the previous 14 sessions; g = min(1, sqrt(H / RV)). Size at entry = g; at each later check size = min(size, g) (shrink only, the
    reduction filled at the next bar's open). Fractional size in the series; Bulenox model at 2 micros: 2 if g >= 0.75, 1 if
    0.25 <= g < 0.75, 0 below.
 V9 No gap adjustment (nikolaosntinas18, SPY, post-hoc there): bands from the 09:30 open alone - upper = open x (1 + sigma), lower =
    open x (1 - sigma).

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE (prop accounts are limited by drawdown): MAR = annualised net return / maximum drawdown of the 16-year path.

    paths, 21-session blocks, seed 20261020): expected 2-year payouts net of activation >= base's and P(account open after 1 year)
