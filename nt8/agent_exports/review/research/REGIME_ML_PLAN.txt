PRE-REGISTERED EXTENSION: A LEARNED MARKET-STATE CLASSIFIER ("PATRICK'S STEP 1 WITH MACHINE LEARNING")
Written 2026-10-20 after REGIME_RESULTS.txt (rule-based classifiers: dead) and before any figure below has been computed.

WHY  Patrick's state call is a model he trained on hundreds of chart screenshots he labelled "trending" or "consolidating", then
on his setups. REGIME_PLAN tested fixed-rule classifiers only. This tests a LEARNED classifier: it is trained on past days labelled
by what they turned out to be, and must predict today's state from what is known at the decision time. His screenshot labels and
his judgement cannot be reproduced; the label below is the numeric version of "this chart trended / it went sideways", and the
features are computed from the same bars a screenshot shows.

LABEL  trend day = the RTH session's efficiency |close - open| / (high - low) is at or above the FIT-period median; otherwise
consolidation. (Hindsight; used only for training and for the oracle.)
FEATURES (causal; 09:30 set known before the open, 10:00 set adds the first 30 minutes - used only with strategies that enter
after 10:00)
  09:30: trend efficiency of the last 5 / 10 / 20 daily closes; body-size ratios 5/60 and 5/20 sessions; overnight range / mean
  RTH range (14); |gap| / its 14-session mean; gap direction x 10-day trend direction; prior-session efficiency; prior range /
  mean range (14); mean efficiency of the last 3 sessions; |overnight move| / mean range; Monday; Friday.
  10:00 adds: first-30-minute efficiency; first-30-minute range / its 14-session mean; |close(10:00)/open - 1| / the 10:00 noise
  sigma (where price sits in noise units); first-30-minute volume / its 14-session mean.
MODELS (numpy, no new packages)  L2 logistic regression (Newton, lambda 1, standardised on the training set, missing -> training
median); gradient-boosted stumps (logistic loss, 150 rounds, learning rate 0.1, 16 quantile thresholds per feature).
POLICIES
  GATE: NoiseArea (published default) only on days with P(trend) >= tau.
  SWITCH: P(trend) >= tau -> NoiseArea; otherwise the best consolidation-style variant (reversion families: overnight fade,
    double top/bottom, band reversion, VWAP stretch, prior-day level fade; with the 10:00 classifier only those entering after
    10:00: double top/bottom, band reversion, VWAP stretch) chosen by t-stat on the fitting window's predicted-consolidation days
    (>= 30 trades, t >= 1; else flat). Catalog defaults or full grid.
  tau {0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6}. Configurations: 2 times x 2 models x (GATE x 4 tau + SWITCH x 2 catalogs x 4 tau) = 48.
SPLITS, COSTS, BENCHMARK, DECISION RULE  exactly as REGIME_PLAN.txt: fit 2010-07..2014, choose the configuration by VALIDATE
(2015..2019-05) Sharpe (ties to the simpler: 09:30 before 10:00, logistic before stumps, GATE before SWITCH, defaults before full,
tau closest to 0.5), refit on the whole discovery period, run once on the 2019-2026 holdout; today's costs; NoiseArea benchmark;
USABLE (self-funded) / USABLE (prop) rules unchanged; the same downgrade checks (2 ticks, years, Databento holdout).
ALSO REPORTED  classifier AUC on VALIDATE and holdout (0.5 = no skill); the ORACLE - the same policies with the true hindsight
label - to show what perfect state knowledge would be worth; all 48 configurations on the holdout (information only).
AUDIT  the logistic model is checked against a plain gradient-descent fit (coefficients within 1e-4), and the holdout series of
the chosen configuration is recomputed from saved predictions by a separate function.
FILES  regime_ml.py -> regime_ml_output.txt; results appended to REGIME_RESULTS.txt.
