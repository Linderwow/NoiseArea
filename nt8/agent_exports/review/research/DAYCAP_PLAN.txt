ENGINE: nav_lab.fam_V (reproduces the live rules exactly when no switch is set - verified again before the run, max diff must be 0).
New switches, all default off:
 cap=N        at most N positions opened per session (an entry from flat or the new leg of a reversal counts as one); once N
              are used, an entry signal from flat is ignored and a reversal signal becomes an exit to flat.
 stop_losses=N  after N trades of the session have CLOSED at a net loss, no new position opens (reversal -> exit). A trade's
              loss = (exit fill - entry fill) x direction < 1.0 point (the modelled round-trip cost at 1 tick + $0.50 a side).
              A reversal whose closing leg is the Nth loss still opens its new leg (only closed trades are known at the decision).
 no_relose    after a trade closes at a net loss (same definition), no new position in THAT direction for the rest of the session;
              the opposite direction stays allowed (a reversal into a blocked direction becomes an exit).
