MARKET DATA DELAY - NOTE   (2026-10-27, read-only measurement; follows LATENCY_NOTE.txt)

BOTTOM LINE
 1. On Mon 10-26 and Tue 10-27 NinjaTrader received MNQ data 17-22 s late for 4-9 minutes right after the 09:30 open, and on
    10-27 again 6.8 s late at 10:36 on ordinary volume. Fri 10-23's open was busier and stayed 0.1 s late.
 2. It is not this PC: 2% busy at the open, NinjaTrader about a third of one core, its busiest thread at most 30% of a core.
    It is not the order path either: a chart order at 09:31:52 was accepted in 31 ms and filled at 29,575.50-75, 14 points from
    the 15-second-old 29,589.25 the chart showed.
 3. Where: during the 10:36 stretch the network card received about 100 bytes per event NinjaTrader took in, the same as when
    on time, while the delay built up and while it drained. The data reached the PC at NinjaTrader's pace, not the exchange's:
    the queue is upstream (Rithmic's paper-trading data server or the route to it), or - less likely with idle threads - a
    small TCP window. The connection's ceiling was about 2,700 events/s (270 KB/s) at the open on 10-26/10-27, about 2,000-2,600
    at 10:36, at least 3,900 on 10-23. Order round trips to Rithmic were the same on 10-23 and 10-26 (median 44 / 43 ms), so a
    fixed window over a changed round trip does not explain Friday; server load or the assigned server may.
 4. The strategy's first decision is 10:00; the open stretches ended 09:34-09:39. 10-26 also ran late 10:00-10:12 (9.7 s at the
    10:00 decision, a hold). A late decision on a Rithmic account goes out late at the then-current price.
 5. ADDED 10-27 12:30 - THE DATA SERVER CHANGED. The feed is Rithmic: NinjaTrader has one live connection, "Bullenox" (type
    "Rithmic for NinjaTrader Brokerage", Rithmic 01 Paper Trading, Chicago); no Tradovate or CQG connection exists (the user
    thought it might be one of those). Rithmic's own log (trace\Rithmic.Bullenox.<date>.000) records the market-data server
    assigned at each login: 96 logins 09-24 to 10-25 morning all got Rithmic's tonpz*.theomne.net / rithmic.com machines; the
    login on Sun 10-25 19:14 ET got host-216-57-156-162.customer.veroxity.net (216.57.156.162, port 40707) - never seen before -
    and the connection has stayed on it since (orders and P&L still on tonpz02020 / tonpz02016). Opens, 09:25-10:15 max delay:
    10-23 on tonpz08020 2.1 s; 10-26 on the new server 22.7 s; 10-27 on the new server 17.8 s. The address is registered to
    TheOmne.net (Rithmic's network); tracert reaches it through Zayo Chicago then Lightower, 45-46 ms, vs 33-40 ms straight
    to tonpz08020 on Zayo. Two days is a correlation, not proof, but it is the only thing that changed and it fits "the data
    reached the PC late". Remedy (the user's): after the close, flat, disconnect and reconnect "Bullenox" - Rithmic assigns
    a server at every login - then check the strategy is enabled; the next open's delay shows whether it was the server.
    `python perfprobe.py --servers` lists the logins; the daily review names the server and warns at 5 s late.
