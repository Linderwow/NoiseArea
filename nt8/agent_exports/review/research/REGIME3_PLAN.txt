REGIME3 - PATRICK'S WAY, PART 3: A LATER CALL, A CHART-SHAPE MODEL, AND A DIRECT "WILL NOISEAREA'S REST OF DAY PAY?" MODEL
Written 2026-10-26 08:30 ET, before any figure below has been computed. Code regime3.py -> regime3_output.txt; results
REGIME3_RESULTS.txt.

WHY  The user (2026-10-24/26) wants Patrick's approach - a market-structure call that decides what to trade that day - built
for us if there is any way, and asked how to cut drawdown. Parts 1-2 (REGIME_RESULTS.txt) called the day at 09:30 or 10:00 from
~18 summary numbers and found no skill (holdout AUC 0.51-0.56). Three things were never tried:
  1. A LATER CALL. By 10:30-12:00 the day has shown more of itself. The question becomes "will the rest of the day trend?".
  2. A CHART-SHAPE MODEL. Patrick's classifier looks at chart pictures. Its numeric equivalent is the raw candles themselves
     (prior 10 daily candles + today's 5-minute path to the call), given to a model that can combine them (depth-2 boosted trees).
  3. A DIRECT TARGET. Instead of a trend/range proxy, predict whether NoiseArea's own trading from the call onward makes money
     (meta-labelling).
Only the GATE policy is tested: on days the call says "range", NoiseArea stands down at the call (an open position exits at the
next bar's open, no new entries that day); otherwise NoiseArea runs untouched. Switching to a reversion strategy is left out:
every reversion family lost in every state in parts 1-2. The gate is also the drawdown lever: it skips the days it expects to lose.

DATA, SPLITS, COSTS  as parts 1-2: NQ 5-min 2010-07 .. 2019-05-05 + MNQ NinjaTrader bars 2019-05-06 .. 2026-10-14 (joined, first
30 valid days of each file dropped); FIT 2010-07-21..2014-12-31, VALIDATE 2015-01-01..2019-05-05, DISCOVERY = both, HOLDOUT
2019-05-06..2026-10-14; secondary holdout on Databento MNQ bars. Costs 1 tick + $0.50 a side per micro (as the published series);
dollars per micro at the reference notional (2 x p_ref) as parts 1-2. NoiseArea = the live rules (nav_lab.fam_V defaults).

CALL TIMES  10:30, 11:00, 12:00 ET (the close of the 5-minute bar ending then; all are NoiseArea check times, so NoiseArea's own
decision at that bar is made first and the gate can only replace it by flat). If the bar is missing or NoiseArea's sigma is NaN,
the gate cannot act that day (NoiseArea runs).

LABELS (hindsight, training only)
  eff : rest-of-day path efficiency |C_close - C_call| / sum |5-min close changes| from the call to the session-close bar is at or
        above its FIT median (per call time) -> "trend" (1), else "range" (0).
  meta: NoiseArea's P&L from continuing minus its P&L when standing down at the call > 0 -> 1, < 0 -> 0. Days where both are equal
        (flat at the call and no later entry) carry no information and are left out of training (the gate does not matter there).
FEATURE SETS (all causal: built only from bars up to and including the call bar and from earlier sessions)
  tab  : the 14 part-2 09:30 features (F930) + at the call: path efficiency open->call; range so far / its 14-session mean at the
         same time; |C_call/O - 1| / sigma(call); volume so far / its 14-session mean at the same time; |NoiseArea position after
         its call decision|; distance beyond the nearer noise-area band in sigma units; |C - VWAP| in sigma units; number of
         NoiseArea position changes so far; |position in today's range - 0.5|.  (23)
  chart: direction-folded raw shape (sign s = sign(C_call - O_0930), +1 if 0): today's path as 12 evenly spaced 5-min closes
         s*(C/O - 1)/sigma(call); the extremes so far in the move's direction and against it, same units; today's gap
         s*(O - prior C)/R14; the prior 10 RTH daily candles as s*body, upper wick, lower wick (wicks swapped when s < 0) and
         s*gap, each / R14 (R14 = mean RTH range of the 14 prior sessions).  (55)
MODELS (numpy)  logit (L2 lambda 1, Newton; part 2's), stumps (150 rounds, lr 0.1, 16 quantile thresholds; part 2's), trees2
  (boosted depth-2 trees, logistic loss, 150 rounds, lr 0.1, the same 16 quantile thresholds, >= 20 training days per leaf).
THRESHOLDS  tau 0.4, 0.5 (stand down when P(class 1) < tau).
CONFIGURATIONS  3 call times x 2 labels x 2 feature sets x 3 models x 2 tau = 72.

SELECTION  each model fitted on FIT, every configuration scored on VALIDATE by Sharpe; the best is chosen (ties -> the simpler:
earlier call, eff before meta, tab before chart, logit < stumps < trees2, tau 0.5 first). Refit on DISCOVERY, run ONCE on HOLDOUT.

DECISION RULE (holdout; NoiseArea alone is the benchmark). The 2019-2026 holdout has been looked at by parts 1-2, so the
significance bar is Bonferroni-tightened over the three regime studies (0.05 / 3):
  USABLE (self-funded): Sharpe > NoiseArea's AND bootstrap P(model Sharpe <= NoiseArea's) < 0.0167 AND t > 2 AND the model's
                        Sharpe also beats NoiseArea's on the Databento holdout.
  USABLE (prop, the drawdown aim): max DD <= 0.6 x NoiseArea's AND $/yr >= 0.6 x NoiseArea's AND 50K pass odds >= NoiseArea's
                        + 10 points (regime_test.prop_odds) AND the Databento holdout max DD is also <= NoiseArea's.
  Either -> NOT live: it goes to the full NAVAR validation gates (new plan) first. Neither -> DEAD ON THIS DATA.
ALSO REPORTED (information only)  AUC on VALIDATE and HOLDOUT for all 36 classifiers; the ORACLE gate per call time (true eff and
meta labels: the ceiling of a perfect later call); all 72 configurations on the holdout; the chosen one at 2 ticks and by year;
how often NoiseArea is in a position at each call time.
AUDITS (must pass before any holdout figure is read)
  A1 the stand-down simulator with the gate never acting reproduces nav_lab.fam_V's points and trades on every session of NQ, MNQ
     and MNQDB (max difference < 1e-9), and the joined series equals nav_lab.series('MNQ', fam_V) exactly.
  A2 causality: for 200 random sessions per file the tab and chart features recomputed from a copy of the day CUT at the call
     bar (later bars deleted) are identical.
  A3 trees2 restricted to depth 1 reproduces stumps' predictions (< 1e-9); logistic Newton vs gradient descent (< 1e-4).
  A4 the chosen holdout series is rebuilt from the saved predictions by separate code (< 1e-12).
Nothing in NinjaTrader or the live strategy changes.
