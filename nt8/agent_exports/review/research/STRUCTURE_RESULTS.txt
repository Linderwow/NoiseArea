  Descriptive, discovery period: NoiseArea did worse after Crabel compression days (Sharpe 0.23 vs 1.00) and better after choppy
  stretches (choppiness top tercile 1.75 vs 0.26 in the trendiest tercile) - the opposite of the folklore, and not stable enough to
  build on (see REGIME_RESULTS.txt: state-to-strategy maps flip between decades).

WHAT THIS MEANS
  Three independent attempts - our catalog with rule-based states, a learned state classifier, and the web's structure methods as
  both setups and states - give the same answer on 16 years of Nasdaq-100 futures: the reversion and "smart money" setups lose, the
  structure states do not predict which strategy will work, and the one robust thing is intraday momentum, which NoiseArea already
  trades in its most validated form. The remaining untested idea is order flow at the bands, which needs tick data.
