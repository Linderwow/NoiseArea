PRE-REGISTERED CROSS-MARKET AND LONG-HISTORY TEST   (written 2026-10-16, before any of the new Databento files is opened)

Question A (diversification): do the SAME rules, unchanged, work on markets whose moves are not Nasdaq's?
Question B (history): did the same rules work on NQ (and ES) in 2010-2019, years the rules have never seen?

Rules: exactly the live strategy. 5-minute bars stamped at close, US cash-session clock (first bar 09:35 ET, checks 10:00-15:30
every 30 minutes, flat on the 16:00 bar, partial sessions flat on the bar before the last), sigma = 14-session average per slot
over the sessions that have the slot (>= 7), VM = 1, Upper/Lower around max/min(09:30 open, previous 16:00 close), VWAP trail,
reversal on the opposite band. No parameter is changed for any market. The clock is deliberately the US equity session for every
market: the question is whether THIS strategy diversifies, not whether some strategy exists on each market.

Data: Databento GLBX.MDP3 ohlcv-1m, continuous front month (.c.0), difference back-adjusted at every contract switch
(same converter as MNQ/MES), 1-min -> 5-min ET bars. Valid session = first RTH bar 09:35 and >= 30 bars.
Costs: 1 tick slippage per side at each market's own tick, commission $0.50/side for micros (MGC, M6E, MCL, MBT, MNQ) and
$2.00/side for full-size contracts (ZN, NQ, ES). 1 contract.
Contract values: MGC $10/pt tick 0.10 | ZN $1000/pt tick 1/64 | M6E $12,500/pt tick 0.0001 | MCL $100/pt tick 0.01 |
MBT $0.10/pt tick 5.00 | NQ $20/pt tick 0.25 | ES $50/pt tick 0.25 | MNQ $2/pt tick 0.25.
Because dollar sizes differ, the comparable figures are the daily-P&L t-stat and the net in units of the market's own
average daily range; dollars are reported but not compared across markets.

A. Per market: discovery = data start .. 2022-12-31 (MCL and MBT from listing), validation = 2023-01-01 .. 2026-10-16.
   PASS = net > 0 in discovery AND net > 0 in validation AND full-period daily t-stat > 2.0.
   Reported alongside, never selected from: VM 0.75 and 1.5; by-year table; correlation of the market's daily strategy P&L
   with MNQ's daily strategy P&L on overlapping days (the diversification question: < 0.3 is what matters).
   Portfolio check (only for passers): equal-risk-scaled sum of daily P&L of MNQ + passers; report t-stat, max drawdown per
   unit of annual profit, versus MNQ alone. This is reported, not a selection.
B. NQ 2010-06-06 .. 2019-05-05 and ES same period, one run each, no variants.
   PASS (NQ) = net > 0 AND daily t-stat > 2.0 over the nine years; by-year table reported. ES is reported for comparison
   with MES 2019-2026 (worked-then-faded vs never-worked).
C. MNQ 2026 update: parity reference only (no test).

Decision rule set now: a market that passes A is a candidate for a second instance of the same strategy on Sim101, after the
same audit and parity steps as MNQ; nothing changes on MNQ either way. A failing NQ 2010-2019 does not stop the live MNQ
strategy but goes into the plan as the strongest known caveat and caps sizing at the prudent cushion until 60 live sessions.
