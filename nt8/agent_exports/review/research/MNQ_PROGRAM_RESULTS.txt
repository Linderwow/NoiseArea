  worst night -106 bp (2024-01-31, -$592), best +226 bp. Without the best 3 nights t 2.88, without the best 5 t 2.47.

  Independent evidence: NQ 2010-2019 +17 bp, t 2.23, 71% winners, 6 of 10 years positive; the published pre-FOMC drift (Lucca and
  Moench, Journal of Finance 2015). Caveats: it was first found on MNQ 2019-2025 (MNQPropBook), so MNQ alone is not a clean test - the
  10 nights since 2025-07 averaged +19 bp, 60% winners; a placebo (the night before FOMC date + 7 days) made +12.6 bp (t 1.63), so
  part of the night effect is general; the pre-FOMC drift has been reported as fading since publication.
  E3 adds the morning drift, which failed on NQ 2010-2019 (-3 bp, t -0.55): E1 alone is the cleaner add-on.
  As coded in NinjaScript (entry at the 16:05 bar open, exit at the RTH open): identical figures, PASS on both sources.
