VERDICT: ALL EIGHT CODABLE STRATEGIES ARE DEAD ON MNQ. Patrick's "99% will be BS" held for 15 of 15 videos. NoiseArea is unchanged.

HOW: 15 popular NQ/MNQ strategy videos found on YouTube; transcripts read from YouTube's own captions in the browser (rules kept, no
transcript text stored). 7 were not codable (paid indicators, one-off discretionary trades, no spoken content, one private video). The
8 codable ones were coded exactly as taught on MNQ 1-minute bars (Databento, 2019-06 .. 2026-04-09, 1,735 sessions), fills simulated
minute by minute, today's costs (1 tick + $0.50 a side). Every one runs with no fitting, so all 7 years are out of sample for us; the
years 2019-2023 come before every video, i.e. out of sample for whatever the authors fitted. Audit: six strategies re-implemented
separately and compared on 150 random sessions - 897 trades, 0 differences; the other two checked structurally.

WHY THE CLAIMS DIFFER: the videos' numbers come from short manual look-backs on the most recent months (Bull Barbie: "this year";
Pasha: 11 months; others: hand-picked examples), win rates that count break-even exits as wins, no costs, and in some cases a paid
indicator to sell. Coded without discretion and run over seven years with costs, the edge is not there. The only rules here with a
long-run edge on MNQ remain NoiseArea's intraday momentum and the FOMC-eve overnight long (MNQ_PROGRAM_RESULTS.txt).

RECORD OF THE PROCESS: the smoke test showed the 9/21 EMA rule as first coded firing ~6 times a day; it was re-coded to the video's
one-entry-per-crossover before any P&L (plan amendment). The first full run had a date-lookup bug in that one strategy (hour index
read against the minute array), visible as $0 in 2019-2022; fixed, logged in the plan, rerun - its verdict did not change, and the
other seven strategies' figures are identical in both runs.
