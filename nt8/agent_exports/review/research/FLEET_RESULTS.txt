FLEET RESULTS (2026-10-24; plan FLEET_PLAN.txt written before the run, amendment 1 added after part A; fleet.py -> fleet_output.txt)

BOTTOM LINE
 - The drastic lever is the NUMBER of funded accounts, not the rules: Bulenox allows 5 funded accounts (FAQ, read 2026-10-24) and
   every one runs the same NoiseArea. With fees paid as they come (A), 5 funded accounts net $13,700 over 2 years vs $3,575 with one
   (3.8x), at 76% vs 81% chance of being ahead - but they share every bad stretch (same signal, same days), need $3,141 out of pocket
   at the median ($7,019 in a bad case), and at half the edge the 5-account plan loses more than $1,000 half of the time.
 - With no budget (A2), the starting money decides more than the cap: $500 covers about one evaluation and a few resets - it is gone
   before a funded account pays in about 2 of 3 paths (P(net > 0) 36% even at the full edge); $1,000 gives 65%. Reinvesting every
   payout into the next evaluation (cap 5) doubles the mean ($6,144 vs $3,023 with $1,000) and ends the two years with 2.1 funded
   accounts open instead of 0.7 - value not counted in the 2-year net, which is why its median looks worse.
 - Taking payouts as soon as allowed stays (B): keeping a $5,000 or $7,500 cushion to trade bigger pays less ($3,014 / $2,518 vs
   $3,534 at the full edge, $795 / $565 vs $1,148 at half) and fewer accounts ever get paid.
 Practical plan, unchanged in kind: start with ONE evaluation (6 micros, resets with 5+ sessions left) and enough set aside for a
 second try (~$1,000 in all); once a funded account pays, put payouts into the next evaluation until 5 funded accounts run, each on
 the cushion rule (1 micro per $2,500, max 4). Nothing in the strategy changes.
