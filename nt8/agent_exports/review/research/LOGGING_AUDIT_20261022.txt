L1-L7  LOW
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
L1 Times are naive Eastern without an offset (trades, events, status, checks, bars, archive, NinjaTrader log). DST changes fall
   on Sunday 02:00, when CME is closed, so there is no practical ambiguity. The trades CSV drops milliseconds (see H2).
   The DB and TapeRecorder use UTC.
L2 Bands are rounded: upper and lower are written "0.00" (NoiseAreaMomentum.cs line 337); sigma has 6 decimals. A close within
   0.005 of a band cannot be re-judged exactly from the CSV. Write bands and VWAP with "R" (round-trip) format.
L3 Files and rows are keyed by "MNQ" (MasterInstrument.Name), not the contract. checks_live has no contract column, so after a
   roll the contract can only be inferred from the date. Add Instrument.FullName.
L4 Two NoiseArea instances on the same account and instrument (e.g. enabled on two charts) would share and overwrite the status,
   events, daystate, checks and bars files. Include the strategy Id in the status, or refuse a second instance.
L5 The scheduled tasks' StartBoundary carries a -04:00 offset, so after 2026-11-01 the 18:20 archive runs at 17:20 EST. This is
   harmless but differs from the description.
L6 The PC is not kept awake. On 10-21 22:57 it was put to sleep by hand (User32 1074), after a crash at 21:22:58. CLAUDE.md says
   sleep is "Never". An overnight sleep delays the 18:20 archive to wake-up (StartWhenAvailable = True, WakeToRun = False) and
   would break a session if it happened during one.
L7 fwd_shadow / archive_bars / daily_review use drop_duplicates('time_et', keep='last'). This is harmless for CME today, but
   duplicated bar times are a symptom worth flagging rather than silently dropping.
