CONSEQUENCES, from the decision rule in LONGHIST_PLAN.txt (16-year figures replace 7-year ones only where worse; the cushion can only rise)
 1. Expectation per micro at today's price: about 8% of notional a year = $4,700 (7-year alone: 11% = $6,200); 1 year in 7 loses;
    a bad year -$2,400 (5th percentile); a great year +$12,000.
 2. Drawdown: worst 14% of notional = $7,900 over 195 sessions, two years to a new high; a within-year drawdown of $5,900 is a
    1-in-20 year and $7,500 a 1-in-100 year.
 3. Cushion per micro, stated as a share of notional so it rises with price: survival minimum 1.5 x worst = 21% of notional =
    $12,000 at today's price (was $6,000); prudent (a repeat of the worst run uses 35-40% of it) = 38% of notional = $21,000
    (was $10,000-12,000). Scale-up arithmetic: $2,000/month needs about 5 micros at today's expectation ($4,700 each) and a
    $105,000 prudent cushion; $5,000/month about 13 micros and $270,000.
 4. The live-vs-backtest 20-session band, the daily review and the gates are unchanged (they test the live code against the
    2019-2026 backtest it was validated on). The 60-clean-sessions-before-sizing rule from MARKETS_RESULTS.txt stands.
 5. Nothing in the rules changes. The whipsaw regime of 2010 is the known failure mode; the defence is the cushion, not a filter.

Side findings while checking the inputs:
 - NinjaTrader's 2019-2026 bars: 14 sessions have 1-9 missing 5-minute bars (thin early-MNQ days in 2019-2020, one 45-minute hole
   on 2026-07-24); 21 of 1,666 reference trades touch them, net -$40. Immaterial.
 - daily_review.py early_closes(): Juneteenth was listed as an early close for 2019-2021, but the cash market only began observing
   it in 2022 (full sessions in the data). Fixed (year >= 2022). The rule now matches every partial session 2015-2026 in two
   independent data sets; 2010-2014 differ because CME closed at 11:30 ET on holidays then and traded full sessions on some
   2012-2014 holidays - a regime that no longer exists. Databento stamps the 13:00/13:15 closing prints one minute later than
   NinjaTrader, which puts a one-bar stub at 13:05/13:20 in the converted files; harmless for every result here.
