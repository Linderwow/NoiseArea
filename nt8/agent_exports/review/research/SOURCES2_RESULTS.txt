second round). Two genuinely new rules pass every pre-registered test - both are "hold NQ overnight" rules, and neither improves
NoiseArea at the strict bar; everything from YouTube failed; refinement never beat luck.
 - C15  hold 1 micro NQ from the RTH close to the next RTH open, only while NQ closes above its 200-day average: +$5,088/yr per micro,
        t 3.96, MNQ-only t 2.58, alpha t 2.56 (the overnight premium, not just market exposure), 14 of 17 years positive (2016 and
        2022 negative), survives 3 ticks a side (t 3.64), zero correlation with NoiseArea. But: worst drawdown $13,300 per micro,
        2023-2026 alone t 1.80, holds 80% of nights, NoiseArea + it P 0.020 (bar 0.01). Independently re-computed: identical.
 - C05  the same overnight hold sized by volatility (min(2, median / current 20-day volatility)): t 3.70, alpha 2.10, P 0.017.
 - W33  Williams %R(5) dip-buy (details below): t 3.76, every year positive, P 0.097.
 - The FOMC-eve add-on (already prepared) is confirmed again on 16 years: plain t 3.78; the three filtered versions that passed

 Not deployable as they stand: none adds to NoiseArea at the pre-registered bar, all hold overnight (prop-account rules unknown), and
