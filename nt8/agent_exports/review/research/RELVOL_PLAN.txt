PRE-REGISTERED TEST: RELATIVE-VOLUME FILTER ON BREAK-OUT ENTRIES   (written 2026-10-19 before any filtered variant was run)

Question: does requiring above-normal volume on the break-out (the 30 minutes up to the decision bar) improve the validated rules
out of sample? Not tested before: the 684-variant sweep's only state filter was first-30-minute efficiency (VARIANTS_PLAN.txt);
the state-machine features F1-F7, the stops, ALPHA2 and GEX tests used no volume; volume only ever entered as the VWAP weight and
in the 3-month order-flow pilot (buy/sell delta from ticks, a different question, still untested for lack of tick history).
Research only: no NinjaScript, parameter or live-strategy change, whatever the result; nothing changes during the 20-session
Sim101 gate. A pass makes a candidate for a separate decision after the gates (NinjaScript, audit, parity), not a rule change.

Disclosed before the plan: the bar files were rebuilt on this PC with convert.py (relvol_data.py, output relvol_data_output.txt)
and only the daily volume series was inspected - no strategy P&L was split by volume. It showed that the Databento files are
calendar-roll continuous (MNQ.c.0, NQ.c.0): in the last sessions before each contract switch they hold the expiring contract,
whose RTH volume is a small fraction of the market's (DB/NT volume ratio, median: 5th-last session 1.00, 4th 0.70, 3rd 0.29,
2nd 0.18, last 0.12; NQ by its own trailing median 0.35/0.22/0.17/0.14/0.09). On every other session Databento and NinjaTrader
volume agree (median ratio 1.000 over 1,486 sessions), so NinjaTrader's history is front-by-volume, like the live feed.

Definition (5-minute bars stamped at close, same session rules as sigma: valid = first RTH bar 09:35 and >= 30 RTH bars)
  V30(k, s)  = volume of session k's RTH bars stamped in (s - 30 min, s], i.e. the six bars ending at the decision bar s
               (defined if session k has the bar stamped s; summed over the bars present).
  AVG(k, s)  = mean of V30(j, s) over the previous 14 eligible valid sessions j < k that have slot s; at least 7 of the 14 must
               have it (as sigma), otherwise RV is undefined.
  RV(k, s)   = V30(k, s) / AVG(k, s). Everything is known at the close of bar s; no look-ahead.
  Roll window (Databento and NQ files only): the 5 valid sessions before each contract switch recorded in the file (the switch is
  the fixed quarterly calendar, known in advance). Those sessions are not eligible for any AVG (the 14-session window skips them and
  reaches further back), and on them the filter is not applied (decisions as the baseline), because their volume is the expiring
  contract's, not the market's. NinjaTrader bars: no roll window; every valid session is eligible and filtered (as live would be).
  Sensitivity, reported only: NT with the Databento roll windows applied on the same dates.

Filter (exits, the VWAP/band trail, the reversal exit and the 16:00 / partial-session flat are unchanged)
  scope E  (entries):            at a check where the rules would open a position from flat, require RV >= theta; otherwise stay
                                 flat (the next check decides afresh).
  scope ER (entries+reversals):  as E, and at a reversal signal the new leg also requires RV >= theta; otherwise the position is
                                 closed and the strategy is flat (a reversal signal always implies the trail exit).
  Undefined RV blocks the entry (never happens where sigma is defined, since both use the same slot rule; counted and reported).
Grid: theta in {0.8, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75} x scope {E, ER} = 10 variants + baseline. Adoptable: theta 1.0 / 1.25 / 1.5 (6
  variants). 0.8 and 1.75 are plateau guards only (0.8 is not "above normal"; neither has a neighbour on both sides).
  Multiple testing: the pick is the best of 6 correlated variants on one discovery set; the guard is the same as in STOPS / ALPHA2
  (a 0.3 t gain on both validation sets, the plateau, NQ 2010-2019), plus the year and top-day conditions below.

Data, periods, costs (as STOPS / ALPHA2 / MARKETS)
  Discovery (the ONLY set used to pick):  Databento MNQ 2019-05..2022-12-31 (mnq_db_5m_2019-2026.csv).
  Same-years cross-check:                 NinjaTrader MNQ 2019-05..2022-12-31 (nt_analyzer_bars.csv).
  Validation:  NT 2023-01-01..2026-10-14 and DB 2023-01-01..2026-10-15 (file end), both.
  Long history: NQ full-size 2010-06..2019-05-05 (nq_2010-2019_5m.csv), period 2010-01-01..2019-05-05 as in MARKETS.
  First 30 valid sessions of every file are warm-up and excluded. 1 contract. MNQ 1 tick + $0.50/side; NQ 1 tick + $2.00/side.
  Fills: the filter adds no new order type - every fill is the validated market order at the next bar's open +/- 1 tick, which is
  the realistic fill for this rule (unlike the stop/target test). Reported only: all sets at 2 ticks/side (a filter that cuts trades
  gains mechanically from higher costs, so this never decides).
  Statistics: net $, daily t-stat (0 on no-trade days), max drawdown, trades, entries blocked, P&L by calendar year, P&L on the
  baseline's best 1% of sessions.

Decision rule, set now. The CANDIDATE is the adoptable variant with the highest Databento discovery daily t-stat. It is adopted only
if ALL of these hold:
  1. Discovery: beats the baseline on net AND t-stat on DB 2019-2022, and on NT 2019-2022.
  2. Validation: beats the baseline on net AND t-stat on NT and on DB, with a t gain of at least 0.3 on both.
  3. Plateau: both one-step theta neighbours (same scope) also beat the baseline on validation net on both datasets.
  4. NQ 2010-2019: beats the baseline on net AND t-stat.
  5. Years: better net than the baseline in a majority of calendar years on each dataset: NT >= 5 of 8 (2019-2026), DB >= 5 of 8,
     NQ >= 6 of 10 (2010-2019). A tie is not better.
  6. Top days: the profit is made on few days (best 1% = 76% of the 2019-2026 net), so the filter may give up at most 10% of it:
     on the baseline's best 1% of sessions (ranked by baseline daily P&L within the period, n = round(1% of sessions), at least 1)
     the candidate keeps >= 90% of the baseline's P&L on those same sessions, in validation on NT and on DB and on NQ 2010-2019.
  If the candidate fails any point, nothing is adopted and no other variant is promoted in its place (the other five are reported,
  not re-selected). A variant that improves drawdown but not net/t is reported, not adopted.
Reported for context, never a pass rule: the mechanism - the baseline's entries bucketed by RV at their decision (< 0.8, 0.8-1,
1-1.25, 1.25-1.5, >= 1.5) with trades and mean P&L per trade, discovery and validation, each dataset.
