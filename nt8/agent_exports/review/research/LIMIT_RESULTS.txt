BOTTOM LINE: keep the market orders. A limit 1 tick better held 60 s LOSES 5.7 ticks a side (95% CI -8.6 to -2.9) against an
immediate market order, in both halves and on the big trend days; delaying the market order by 5 / 15 / 30 s loses 7.5 / 3.4 / 1.7.
The pilot's +1.7-tick saving (62 entries, early 2026) does not survive seven years. The real cost of the 10:00 market entries is
+2.6 ticks a side, not the backtest's 1 tick - about $54 a year per micro, small next to the strategy's ~$4,700 - and it rises
steeply with latency (+6.2 ticks at 150 ms), so keeping the order path fast matters more than the order type.
