CONSEQUENCES (from the decision rule written in MARKETS_PLAN.txt before the test)
   1. No second instance of the strategy on any other market. MNQ only.
   2. NQ 2010-2019 failing the net bar is now the strongest known caveat: it goes into the plan and the spec, and sizing stays at
      1 micro with the prudent cushion ($10-12k) until 60 clean live sessions.
   3. The live MNQ strategy is not stopped: 2019-2026 validation stands (every full year positive, parity 1,666/1,666), and the
      long history shows the gross signal existed in 8 of the 9 earlier years at a size that today's lower relative costs can carry.
   4. What the long history adds to expectations: the profit arrives in bursts (2018, 2020, 2022, 2025). Calm years can be flat or
      slightly negative net. Judge the live strategy on >= 12 months, not on a quiet quarter.
   5. Do not spend more on MGC/ZN volume-roll files: the FX and bond results are decided by costs vs range (ZN costs $35 per trade on
      a $389 average daily range), and gold's valid-session result is already negative gross. Bitcoin (MBT) is the only market with a
      gross signal; it is not tradable at 1 micro after costs and is logged for a future look if MBT's tick or liquidity changes.
