WHAT THIS MEANS (all three regime studies together: 24 + 48 + 72 configurations)
  - A day-type call built from price charts cannot be reproduced here; every honest version is a coin flip out of sample. The
    chart-shape model shows exactly how a screenshot-trained classifier fails: near-perfect on the days it learned, chance after.
  - NoiseArea's own "wait until price leaves the noise area" is still the only state detector that pays.
  - Untested and not testable yet: order flow at the open (ticks only since 2025-10-26 - about 250 sessions, too few to fit and
    check a classifier; the TapeRecorder keeps collecting), and Patrick's own day-by-day calls. If he shares his calls,
    time-stamped before each decision, regime_test.py / regime3.py score them against these labels in an afternoon - the one test
    that would settle whether his model sees something ours cannot.
