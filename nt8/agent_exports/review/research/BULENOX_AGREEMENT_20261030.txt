WHAT WE DO ABOUT IT (2026-10-30)
1. Inactivity (Exhibit A): "at least one (1) trade per five (5) trading days ... prohibited to be inactive for more than five (5)
   trading days"; violation = suspension without notice and the balance forfeited; "artificial activity intended only to prevent
   inactivity does not satisfy this requirement". NEW: was "one trade per week" in our model. NoiseArea has 2.7 dry runs of >= 5
   sessions a year -> without a fix 79% of Masters die within a year (INACTIVITY_RESULTS.txt). ADOPTED: activity fallback K=3 V=0
   (staged in NoiseAreaMomentum / NoiseAreaStrategy; the daily review counts rolling trading days, not calendar weeks).
2. Same direction across accounts (s.14): all equity-index positions (ES/NQ/YM/RTY/EMD and micros) across all Master accounts
   "must be maintained and executed in a uniform directional manner"; violation = termination of the account; Bulenox MAY aggregate
   Qualification accounts, same Rithmic user, same IP/device/household. NEW. Staged: NoiseAreaStrategy's same-direction guard holds
   an entry back while another non-simulation account on the connection holds the other side, and sends it once that side is flat.
   THE OTHER HALF IS MANUAL: a hand trade against an open NoiseArea position (the user trades MNQ by hand on BX49277-97/-98/-99)
   breaks the rule from the manual side; the strategy cannot prevent that. Flat before trading the other way, or trade WITH it.
3. Flipping (s.7): <= 20% of trading days in a payout period may look like placeholder days; examples include end-of-day check-in
   trades, seconds-long trades, size cuts after a big day. The fallback's sessions are 7.7% of trade days and are full NoiseArea
   trades (same size, 10:00-15:30 checks, VWAP exit, 16:00 flat); nothing "creates a day" at the close. Never add a manual
   round trip to satisfy the inactivity rule (the 2026-10-24 review text said so - removed).
4. Average position size (s.7): "must have an average position size that does not exceed the average during the qualification
   process". The funded cushion rule (1 micro, +1 per $2,500 cushion, max 4) stays below a 6-micro NoiseArea evaluation and below
   the manual evaluation's average (BX49277-99: 1.85 per entry fill, 4.5 peak per round trip, 17 round trips 10-26..10-29).
5. Funded Account transition (s.13): after three Rewards the Master becomes eligible for a LIVE Funded Account (separate agreement,
   not in our possession); declining closes the Master. NEW. Our Master models' payouts after the third are therefore outside the
   Master - reported as "first 3" in INACTIVITY_RESULTS. Not modelled further: no Funded Account rules in hand (never invent them).
