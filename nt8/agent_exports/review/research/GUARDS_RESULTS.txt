     with a note naming both positions, an alert, an event-log line, then SetState(State.Terminated). NinjaTrader's own
     exit-on-session-close and error handling can then no longer trade the stale bookkeeping into an opposite position. The
     Terminated handler keeps state "desync" in the heartbeat. One bar of grace remains for a fill in progress, as before.

     that session's historical replay, so the start-up "synchronize account" cannot re-enter the position the halt closed;
     (c) the day state is also saved on every real-time position change (OnPositionUpdate) and at termination.
 G4  Append-only event log agent_exports\NoiseArea_events_<inst>_<acct>.txt: live start, desync, desync cleared, desync stop,
     halt, halt restored, history alert, termination (ET time | event | account | detail). Live runs only.
