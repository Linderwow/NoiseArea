DEFINITIONS (as EXEC_PILOT_PLAN sections 3-4, Part B rules):
 T = 10:00:00.000 ET. t0 = the first trade at or after T (NinjaTrader closes the bar on the first tick after its end). Order arrives
 at t1 = t0 + latency; PRIMARY latency 75 ms (LATENCY_NOTE: 70-90 ms bar close to exchange with Rithmic); sensitivity 25 and 150 ms.
 R = the first trade at or after T = the backtest's fill (next bar's open); the backtest charges R + 1 tick.
 Touch at time t rebuilt from the last trade with a known side at or before t: buyer at p -> ask p, bid p - 1 tick; seller at p ->
 bid p, ask p + 1 tick. A market buy fills at the ask, a sell at the bid. Cost per side in ticks vs R, positive = worse.
 MARKET (current): cost = touch at t1.
 LIMIT (the pilot's best, the only variant tested): L = C - s x 1 tick (C = the last trade before T, s = +1 buy / -1 sell), sent at
 t1, held to T + 60 s. CONSERVATIVE fill (primary): at once at the touch if marketable at t1; otherwise at L only if a trade prints at
 least 1 tick through L after t1 and before T + 60 s; otherwise cancelled and sent at market at T + 60 s (cost = touch then) - every
 entry still gets filled, so a miss costs a price difference, never a lost trade. OPTIMISTIC bound: fills at L on any print at L.
 SAVING = market cost - limit cost (ticks a side; positive = the limit is cheaper).

DECISION RULES (fixed now)
 PROMISING (worth building a limit-entry option, its Analyzer parity and a Sim forward test - after the 20-clean-session gate):
   conservative mean saving at 75 ms >= +0.5 tick a side, its 95% bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples of entries, seed 20261020) above 0,
   positive in 2019-2022 and in 2023-2026 separately, and the mean saving on the entries of the trades that ran furthest (top 20% by
   final points) not below -2 ticks (waiting must not systematically miss the big trend days).
 COST-MODEL CHECK: the market cost at 75 ms vs the backtest's 1 tick; reported with CI and by year; > 1.5 ticks = the live gate's
   threshold is breached at 10:00 (a finding for the cost model; nothing changes in the code).
 Value statement: saving x $0.50 a tick x the 10:00 entries a year (~65), per micro.
CHECKS: R equals the open of the MNQ 1-minute bar starting 10:00 from the caches (same contract period); three entries worked by hand;
cost signs on a synthetic case; window completeness (every window has trades on both sides of T).
Results: LIMIT_RESULTS.txt, raw limit_output.txt, script limit_test.py.
