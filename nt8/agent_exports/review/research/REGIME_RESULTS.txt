WHAT WAS TESTED
  Catalog: 9 families, 732 variants - noise-area momentum, opening-range breakout, overnight-range fade, double top/bottom, band
  reversion, VWAP-pullback continuation (the 684-variant sweep) plus three new: VWAP-stretch mean reversion, prior-day high/low
  (fade and break - "areas of interest"), opening gap (fade and follow).
  State classifiers known at 09:30: 10-day trend efficiency (trending vs ranging), 5/60-day volatility ratio (expanding vs
  contracting), both together (state then structure, 9 cells), overnight range x gap size, prior-day type; plus "no state".
  Selection: per state the best variant (or best two) by t-stat on past data, no trade if t < 1. 24 configurations; fitted on
  2010-2014, chosen on 2015-2019 (NQ), run once on 2019-05 .. 2026-10 (MNQ, NinjaTrader bars). Today's costs throughout.

DISCOVERY (choice of model)
  Fitted on 2010-2014 the full-catalog switchers reached Sharpe 2.3-4.0 and fell to 0.13-0.54 on 2015-2019 - the overfitting that
  "pick the best strategy per state from a large catalog" invites. The defaults catalog held up better (1.0-1.4). The registered rule
  chose overnight range x gap (S4), defaults, two at a time: validation Sharpe 1.359, against 1.358 for the 10-day trend split (S1,
  defaults, one at a time) and 1.09 for NoiseArea.
  Each family's default over 2010-2019 at today's costs: momentum +$3,500/yr (Sharpe 0.88), opening-range breakout +$2,605 (0.62),
  opening gap +$980 (0.39), VWAP pullback +$485 (0.29); every reversion family lost (overnight fade -$472, prior-day level fade
  -$748, band reversion -$922, double top/bottom -$951, VWAP stretch -$1,633).

WHY IT FAILS (regime_diag.py, descriptive)
  What persists from 2010-2019 to 2019-2026 is the family ranking - momentum and breakout win in every state, reversion loses in
  every state (rank correlation of the 732 variants 0.63 with no states at all; conditioning on a state does not raise it, and in
  the "strongly trending" tercile it falls to 0.03). What does not persist is which state suits which strategy: NoiseArea's own
  results by state flip between periods (e.g. low overnight range and small gap: Sharpe -1.41 in 2010-2019, +2.77 in 2019-2026 -
  the cell the model switched off earned $2,026/yr in the holdout). The same instability is why the 2026-10-16 state machine found
  no usable real-time predictor.

WHAT THIS MEANS
  - Keep NoiseArea as it is. A regime switcher built from these pieces is worse on return, risk-adjusted return, drawdown and prop
    pass odds.
  - The catalog result is itself useful: on Nasdaq-100 futures intraday, momentum/breakout styles earn and the reversion styles lose,
    in every state tested. A Golden-Gate-style low-drawdown model would need edges this catalog does not have (order flow at levels
    is still untested), not a smarter switch between these ones.
  - If Patrick's per-state rules are ever shared, they can be run through the same harness (regime_test.py) in an afternoon.

THE CENTRAL FINDING
  The market state is worth a fortune - if you know it. With the true (hindsight) label NoiseArea earned +$15,509/yr on trend days
  and -$9,329/yr on consolidation days; trading only true trend days gives Sharpe 3.7 with a $1,379 worst drawdown, and switching to
  a reversion strategy on true consolidation days Sharpe 5.2 ($25,461/yr). That is the kind of curve Golden Gate shows.
  But the state cannot be seen in advance from the bars: classifier skill (AUC, 0.5 = coin flip) is 0.51-0.52 at 09:30 and
  0.55-0.56 at 10:00 on the holdout, and the in-sample 0.60-0.66 does not survive. A 0.56 classifier keeps most of the cost of
  the bad days and throws away many of the good ones.
  NoiseArea already contains the only state detector that worked here: it waits until price has left the noise area, i.e. until the
  day has shown it is trending, and pays a small loss when that turns out wrong.

FOR PATRICK'S MODEL SPECIFICALLY
  A classifier trained on screenshots learns whatever the screenshot shows. If a labelled chart includes bars after the moment the
  decision would have been made, the model learns to recognise a trend day in hindsight - which looks exactly like the oracle above
  (Sharpe 3-5, tiny drawdowns) in a backtest and does not hold live. He says he selected "causal cases"; the test of that is his
  live record against his backtest, which he reports as matched for two versions. If he ever shares his model's day-by-day state
  calls (time-stamped before the decision), regime_test.py can score them against this label and run them through the same switch.
