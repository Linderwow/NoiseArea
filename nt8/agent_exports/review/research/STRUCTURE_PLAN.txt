PRE-REGISTERED TEST: MARKET-STRUCTURE METHODS FROM THE WEB, AS SETUPS AND AS STATE DETECTORS
Written 2026-10-20 after REGIME_RESULTS.txt (Parts 1-2: dead) and before any figure below has been computed. Live strategy unchanged.

WHY  The regime test used our own catalog and generic state measures. This adds the market-structure methods traders actually use,
taken from public descriptions (Market Profile / auction theory per Steidlmayer and Dalton; ICT / smart-money concepts; Wyckoff;
Crabel; the academic intraday-momentum paper of Gao, Han, Li and Zhou, JFE 2018; variance-ratio and choppiness regime measures).
Where sources disagree, the most common reading is coded below and stated; no rule is tuned after seeing results.
Already tested earlier and not repeated: volume-profile setups (242 trials, MNQPropBook, all failed), the opening drive
(MNQOpenDrive, failed: t 2.25 vs 3.71); the 5-minute opening-range breakout (family B) and overnight fade (C) are in the catalog.

SAME HARNESS AS REGIME_PLAN.txt  files, joined NQ 2010-2019 (discovery) / MNQ NinjaTrader bars 2019-2026 (holdout) and Databento MNQ
(secondary holdout); gross P&L converted to return on the raw 09:30 price less one tick + $0.50 per side at today's price; FIT
2010-07..2014, VALIDATE 2015..2019-05, refit on discovery, ONE holdout run; NoiseArea benchmark; month-block bootstrap; 50K evaluation
odds; robustness at 2 and 4 ticks, by year, Databento. Signals at a bar close fill at the next bar open; stops fill at the stop
(or the open if it gaps through); limits fill at the limit only when traded through by a tick; flat at the 15:55 close.
Levels: ON = overnight high/low (18:00-09:30), PD = prior RTH high/low, OR30 = 09:30-10:00 range, IB = 09:30-10:30 range,
VA = prior RTH value area (70% of volume around the point of control; each 5-minute bar's volume spread evenly over its range;
0.25-point bins below price 8,000, 1-point bins above), a = ATR(14) of 5-minute bars at the signal bar.

NEW SETUP FAMILIES (62 variants; "defaults" = the first value of every list)
 J Dalton open type, classified at the 10:00 close from the first six bars: OPEN DRIVE = never more than tol*a through the open on
   the far side, closes in the outer quarter of the 30-minute range beyond the open; TEST-REVERSE (open-test-drive / open-
   rejection-reverse merged; 5-minute bars cannot separate them) = probed tol*a or more one side, closes beyond the open on the other
   side in the outer third; otherwise OPEN AUCTION (no trade). Follow the drive at 10:05. Stop: the open -/+ 0.5a ("back through the
   open voids it") or the 30-minute extreme; exit at the close or when a bar closes back through VWAP.
   types {drive + test-reverse, drive only} x tol {0.5, 1.0} x stop {open, extreme} x exit {close, vwap}            16
 K Initial balance, 10:35-14:00, first event only. break: close beyond IB high/low by 0.1a -> follow, stop IB mid or opposite side;
   fade: a bar trades beyond and closes back inside -> fade, stop the excursion extreme + 0.25a, target IB mid or opposite side.
   mode {break, fade} x stop/target {mid, opposite} x exit {close, 2R} (break; for fade: the target itself) x filter {none, narrow
   IB < 0.8 x its 14-session mean}                                                                                      16
 L 80% rule: opens outside the prior VA; once price is back inside, h consecutive half-hour closes (10:00, 10:30, ...) inside the VA
   -> trade to the far VA edge (target), stop s*a beyond the near edge; signal by 13:00. h {2, 1} x s {0.5, 1.0}         4
 M ICT Silver Bullet: from 09:30 a level is swept (traded through); between 10:05 and 11:00 a 3-bar fair value gap forms against the
   sweep (short after a high sweep: low[i-2] > high[i]); a limit at the gap edge nearest price (high[i] for a short) works until
   11:30; stop one tick beyond the session extreme at entry; target 2R, 3R or the close.
   levels {ON + PD, ON, PD} x target {2R, 3R, close}                                                                   9
 N Liquidity sweep / Wyckoff spring-upthrust, 10:05-14:00, one per side: a bar trades through the level and closes back inside by
   buf*a -> fade at the next open, stop 0.25a beyond the bar's extreme, target VWAP, the opposite level or 2R.
   levels {OR30, ON} x target {vwap, opposite, 2R} x buf {0, 0.25}                                                     12
 O Market intraday momentum (Gao et al.): r = prior close -> 10:00 return; if |r| >= thr x its 14-session mean, enter in r's
   direction at the next open after the entry bar, exit at the last RTH close. thr {0, 0.5} x entry bar {15:30, 15:00}  4

   filter on the prior session {none, NR4, NR7, inside day, inside day + NR4}                                         5

TESTS

     EDGE = discovery t >= 2 and holdout t >= 2.45 (5% one-sided, Bonferroni for 7 families). ADDITION = NoiseArea + that variant
     (1 micro each) beats NoiseArea's Sharpe with month-block bootstrap P < 5%.
 (B) SWITCHER: catalog = the 732 earlier variants + these 62 (defaults catalog = one default per family, 16); 28 configurations
