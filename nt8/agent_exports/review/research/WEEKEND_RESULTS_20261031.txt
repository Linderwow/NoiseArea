WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE TWO QUESTIONS
  1. Patrick's mechanism. Four studies (REGIME, REGIME_ML, REGIME3, this) and ~160 configurations now cover price shape, volume, VIX,
     gamma, structure, raw candles, chart analogs, a second index and order flow at the open. The day type is worth 2-3x NoiseArea in
     hindsight and is not visible in advance from anything on hand. The only thing left that could settle whether his model sees more is
     his own day-by-day calls, time-stamped before each decision (patrick_calls.py). A model trained on screenshots that include the
     rest of the day learns hindsight; that is the first thing to check if he shares them.
  2. Drawdown. Every rule that lowers the 16-year drawdown here does it by trading less (long-only, caps, filters, the S&P gate, the
     phase blend) and pays for it in return and Sharpe, or by cutting winners (targets, scale-out, trails) and fails on other windows.
     The drawdown that matters for a Bulenox account is managed by size (MASTERSCALE: 1 micro per $2,500 cushion) and by operations;
     the strategy's own drawdown is the price of its payoff shape (all the money comes from the ~90 sessions a year that move more
     than a daily sigma). The chandelier on Sim101 is a modest, unproven improvement of that shape; K1 + chandelier would be a larger
     one if it were real, and the data cannot show that it is.
