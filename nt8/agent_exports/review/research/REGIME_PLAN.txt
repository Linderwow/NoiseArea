PRE-REGISTERED TEST: A REGIME-SWITCHING STRATEGY CATALOG ("PATRICK'S WAY") vs NOISEAREA
Written 2026-10-20, before any regime, catalog or switcher figure below has been computed. Nothing in the live strategy changes.

QUESTION
Patrick (Golden Gate) describes: (1) recognise the market state (trending vs consolidating), (2) evaluate its structure, (3) pick
from a catalog of ~10 strategies (momentum, breakout, mean reversion, reversion to areas of interest) the one that has worked best
in that state, (4) trade only if style and risk are acceptable. Golden Gate's own rules are not known. This tests the DESIGN with
our data and a catalog of our own: can a causal state classifier plus per-state strategy selection beat - or usefully de-risk -
NoiseArea out of sample? Answer one of: USABLE (self-funded), USABLE (prop), USABLE AS OVERLAY, or DEAD ON THIS DATA.

DATA AND SPLITS (one continuous 16-year series, as in LONGHIST/SCALING)
  NQ 5-min, research\nq_2010-2019_5m.csv (Databento, back-adjusted continuous), sessions up to 2019-05-05   -> DISCOVERY
  MNQ 5-min, research\nt_analyzer_bars.csv (NinjaTrader's own bars), sessions from 2019-05-06 to 2026-10-14 -> HOLDOUT
  Secondary holdout for data robustness: research\mnq_db_5m_2019-2026.csv (Databento MNQ), same dates.
  First 30 valid sessions of every file are warm-up (research_engine.WARMUP), as everywhere else.
  Discovery is split for model choice: FIT 2010-07-21 .. 2014-12-31, VALIDATE 2015-01-01 .. 2019-05-05. The final model is refit on
  the whole discovery period and run ONCE on the holdout. The holdout is not looked at before that single run.
UNITS AND COSTS
  Every variant runs gross (no slippage, no commission, 1 point = 1). Daily return = points / raw 09:30 price (research\
  raw_open930.csv; the bar open where missing), less 2 sides x 0.5 / p_ref per round trip, p_ref = mean 2026 09:30 open: one tick
  plus $0.50 commission per side at today's price, i.e. today's costs throughout ("cost basis (b)", identical to scaling_daily.csv).
  Dollars are per micro at today's notional N = 2 x p_ref. Cost sensitivity: x2 and x4 (2 and 4 ticks per side) on the final model.

CATALOG (9 families, 732 variants; signal at a bar close fills at the next bar open, flat by the last RTH bar)
  A noise-area momentum (432, research_engine.fam_A)        B opening-range breakout (96, fam_B)
  C overnight-range fade (36, fam_C/onfade)                 D double top/bottom at HOD/LOD -> VWAP (72, fam_D)
  E noise-area band reversion (24, fam_E)                   F VWAP-pullback continuation (24, fam_F)
  NEW, written for this test (regime_test.py), to cover Patrick's list:
  G VWAP-stretch mean reversion (12): 10:30-15:00, a close more than k x ATR(5-min,14) from VWAP is faded at the next open, stop
    s x ATR beyond the signal close, target the VWAP at the signal; k {2, 3, 4} x s {1, 2} x max trades/day {1, 2}.
  H prior-day high/low, "areas of interest" (24): 09:45-15:00, mode fade (a bar trades through the prior RTH high/low and closes
    back inside by buf x ATR -> fade, stop s x ATR beyond the level) or break (a close beyond the level by buf x ATR -> follow, stop
    s x ATR back inside); buf {0, 0.25} x s {1, 2} x exit {VWAP (target for fade, close-through exit for break), 2R, close}.
  I opening gap (12): gap = 09:30 open - prior RTH close; if |gap| >= g x its 14-session mean, at the 09:35 close either fade it
    (target the prior close) or follow it (exit at the close); stop s x ATR beyond the 09:35 extreme; g {0.5, 1.0, 1.5} x mode x s {1, 2}.
  "defaults" catalog = one pre-chosen variant per family (function defaults; G k3 s1 n1, H fade buf0.25 s1 VWAP, I g1.0 fade s1).
  "full" catalog = all 732.
STATE / STRUCTURE CLASSIFIERS (causal: everything is known at 09:30 from prior sessions and the overnight session)
  ER10  efficiency of the last 10 daily RTH closes: |C(-1) - C(-11)| / sum |daily changes|          (trending vs ranging)
  VOLR  mean |C - O| of the last 5 sessions / of the last 60 sessions                                 (volatility expanding vs contracting)
  ONR   overnight range (18:00-09:30) / mean RTH range of the last 14 sessions                         (overnight structure)
  GAPZ  |09:30 open - prior close| / its 14-session mean                                               (opening structure)
  PDE   prior session |C - O| / (H - L)                                                                (prior day trend day vs range day)
  Cut points (terciles; GAPZ median) are fixed on the FIT period (on the whole discovery period for the final model) and never
  re-estimated on later data. Schemes:
    S0 one cell (no regime; "just pick the best strategy")         S1 ER10 (3 cells)        S2 VOLR (3)
    S3 ER10 x VOLR (9 cells, state then structure)                 S4 ONR x GAPZ (6)        S5 PDE (3)
SELECTION ("the best one for the state, statistically")
  In every cell, among variants with at least 30 trades on that cell's days in the fitting window, take the highest t-stat of the
  variant's daily net return over the cell's days (0 on days it does not trade). If that t is below 1.0 the cell does not trade
  ("risk not acceptable"). K = 1 (that variant) or K = 2 (best two from different families, half weight each: "one or two at a time").
  24 configurations = 6 schemes x 2 catalogs x 2 K. The configuration with the best annualised Sharpe on VALIDATE (fitted on FIT) is
  the model; ties to the simpler one. It is then refitted on the full discovery period and run once on the holdout.
  Also reported, for information only: every configuration on the holdout; the same switcher with the cell selection made on the
  holdout itself (ORACLE, pure hindsight - an upper bound of what state-based selection could give with this catalog); and the
  OVERLAY: NoiseArea always plus, per cell, the best non-A variant if its t >= 1.0.
BENCHMARK  NoiseArea = fam_A published default (VM 1.0, 14 days, every 30 min, paper trail), same data, same costs.
DECISION RULE (holdout only)
  USABLE (self-funded): model Sharpe > NoiseArea Sharpe, paired month-block bootstrap (10,000) P(difference <= 0) < 5%, and model
    daily t > 2.
  USABLE (prop): model worst drawdown <= 60% of NoiseArea's, annual return >= 60% of NoiseArea's, and simulated 50K evaluation pass
    odds (1 micro, $3,000 target, $2,500 end-of-day trailing drawdown that stops trailing at the starting balance - the rules
    recorded in LONGHIST_RESULTS.txt; 4,000 month-block paths of the holdout, 24-month cap) >= NoiseArea's + 10 points.
  USABLE AS OVERLAY: overlay Sharpe > NoiseArea Sharpe with bootstrap P < 5%.
  Otherwise DEAD ON THIS DATA (for this catalog and these classifiers; Golden Gate's own rules are untested).
  Whatever the verdict, the final model also gets: costs x2/x4, best 10%/15% of days removed, by-year results, Monte Carlo drawdown,
  longest gap between trades, and the secondary (Databento) holdout. A positive verdict that fails any of these at 2 ticks per side,
  or is negative in more than 2 of the 7 holdout years, is downgraded to "promising, not proven".
AUDITS BEFORE ANY RESULT IS READ

  (2) G, H, I re-implemented independently as plain loops agree trade for trade on 200 random days of both files;
  (3) the switcher's holdout figures are recomputed by an independent script from the saved daily matrices.
FILES  regime_test.py (runner) -> regime_output.txt, REGIME_RESULTS.txt; regime_check.py (audits 2-3).

AMENDMENT 2026-10-20 (before any figure was computed)
  - Files: the NQ and MNQ files are the exact copies the scaling test used (scaling_inputs\nq_2010-2019_5m.csv and
    scaling_inputs\nt_analyzer_bars_to_2026-10-14.csv), so audit (1) can be exact; the secondary holdout is unchanged.
  - The NQ file ends 2019-05-03 and the MNQ file starts 2019-05-05, so the classifiers run on the joined daily series (NQ sessions,
    then MNQ sessions); the close-to-close change and the gap across the join are left out (they mix two price series). Everything
    in points cancels the back-adjustment offsets.
  - Overnight-range fade (C): its 75-point stop cap was set for MNQ near 12,500; here it is the scale-free equivalent, skip if the
    stop is more than 0.6% of price (onfade's max_stop_pct), so it means the same in 2010 and 2026.
  - The D default (tol 0.15) is not in the D grid; it is added as one extra column used only by the "defaults" catalog.
  - K = 2: the best two qualifying variants from different families share the cell equally; a cell where only one qualifies
    trades that one at full weight.
  - Cost sensitivity is reported as 1 / 2 / 4 ticks of slippage per side plus $0.50 commission; "at 2 ticks" in the downgrade rule
    means 2 ticks per side.
