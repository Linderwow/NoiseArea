WHAT IS NEW HERE (each item states why it was never tried)
  A. Day-type recognition with information not used before
   A1 ANALOG DAYS (k-nearest-neighbour chart matching). "Recognize the chart pattern" the way a person does: today's first 30/60
      minutes (5-minute closes relative to the 09:30 open, in units of that slot's noise sigma) plus the shape of the previous two
      sessions and the overnight gap are matched against every EARLIER session; the call is the share of the K nearest neighbours
      that turned out to be trend days. Non-parametric, walk-forward (neighbours strictly before the day), no fitted model that can
      memorise. REGIME3 used boosted trees on candles (memorised, AUC 0.5 after); kNN analogs were never run.
      Hypothesis: days whose opening path resembles past trend days trend more often. Payer: none specific (a day-type call earns
      nothing by itself; it only decides whether NoiseArea trades). Labels as REGIME3: eff = rest-of-day efficiency |C_last - C_call| /
      (H - L after the call) >= the fit-period median; meta = NoiseArea's own daily P&L (points) > 0 (no-trade days undefined).
      Primary: call 10:30, K = 50, label eff. Reported, not selected: 10:00 call; K = 25 / 100; label meta.
      PASS: AUC >= 0.60 on VALIDATE (2015-01 .. 2019-05, NQ) AND on HOLDOUT (2019-05 .. 2026-10, MNQ NinjaTrader bars), else dead.
      If it passes, the REGIME3 stand-down gate is run once at tau 0.5 and must meet REGIME3's prop rule.
   A2 SECOND INDEX (ES 2010-2019, MES 2019-2026, both on hand, never used as information for NoiseArea). At each NoiseArea check the
      S&P's own noise-area state is known: has ES/MES also closed outside ITS band in the same direction (same rules, own sigma)? and
      the relative strength NQ-vs-ES since the open. Hypothesis: a Nasdaq breakout confirmed by the broad market is more often a
      trend day (index-wide flow) than a Nasdaq-only move; payer: the same as NoiseArea's (participants forced to chase an index-wide
      move). Two trials, fixed now: A2a skip an entry when ES is not outside its band on the same side; A2b size 2 units when ES
      confirms, 1 when not (per-unit MAR). Also reported: AUC of "ES confirms at 10:00" for the eff / meta labels (day-type use).
   A3 ORDER FLOW AT THE OPEN (NinjaTrader's own MNQ tick store, exported 2025-10-27 .. 2026-10-29, 265 sessions, every print with
      the aggressor side). Features from 09:30 to the call (09:45 reported, 10:00 primary): F1 signed volume imbalance (at-ask minus
      at-bid volume over total); F2 large-print imbalance (prints of 10+ contracts); F3 trade intensity (prints per minute over the
      median of the previous 14 sessions at that time); F4 imbalance persistence (lag-1 autocorrelation of 1-minute signed volume);
      F5 absorption (price change in ticks per 1,000 contracts of net aggressor volume; small = heavy passive side). Labels eff and
      meta as A1. Univariate AUC with a 95% bootstrap CI (2,000 resamples), 10 looks at 10:00 (5 features x 2 labels); a feature
      passes only if AUC >= 0.60 and its CI excludes 0.50 at Bonferroni 10 (99.5% CI). Also reported: a 2-feature logistic
      leave-one-session-out AUC (F1 + F3). POWER, declared: 265 sessions give SE(AUC) ~ 0.035 - a true 0.60 is detectable at about
      2.8 SE, a true 0.55 is not; a negative result here says "no strong signal", not "no signal".
