   (~6 activations per slot in 2 years). The AVERAGE is high because a minority of runs pay very large; the typical result is no
   better, the chance of being ahead falls, the bad cases and the cash needed rise a lot. At half the edge it is a coin flip.
 - At the user's prices a middle size, FUNDED AT 2 MICROS, beats the cushion plan (MASTERSCALE, chosen at $175/$148 without
   replacement) on both the typical and the average result, at a somewhat lower chance of being ahead and about twice the cash.
