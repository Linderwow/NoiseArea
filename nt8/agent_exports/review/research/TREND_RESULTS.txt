6. DATA FINDING FROM THE AUDIT (affects any Databento-based study that holds positions over expiry)
   Databento's continuous front-month (.c.0) keeps the expiring contract through expiry day, and that contract stops trading at
   09:30, so every expiry day has NO session in the Databento files: all 30 of 2019-2026 (29 Fridays and Thursday 2026-06-18, the
   Juneteenth expiry) and 35 of 36 in NQ 2010-2019. The move from expiry morning to the Sunday reopen is then folded into the roll
   gap. NinjaTrader rolls about a week earlier and has those days. Size over 2019-2026, checked against NinjaTrader: -13.4% of
   notional for a long position (largest -7.9%, the 2020-03-20 roll weekend), i.e. the Databento
   series flatters a long position by about 1.8% a year there. Patched with the cash index (FRED NASDAQ100; error per roll falls from
   1.85% to 0.51% against NinjaTrader): discovery B&H 0.964 vs best trend 0.959 (TS21-LF) - still none eligible, and had TS21-LF
   qualified, its only neighbour (TS63-LF) loses to B&H on both validation datasets, so the plateau test fails. Verdict unchanged.
   For NoiseArea (always flat at 16:00) these are simply about 4 missing sessions a year in the Databento backtests, not mis-stated ones.
   Also missing from the Databento MNQ files: 2025-12-31 (the boundary between the 2023-2025 and 2026 purchases; a Databento end date
   is exclusive). Harmless for daily returns here; one missing NoiseArea session.
