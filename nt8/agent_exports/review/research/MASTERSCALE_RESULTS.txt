BOTTOM LINE - ADOPTED by the plan's rule: in the funded Bulenox 50K Master, trade 1 micro per $2,500 of cushion (balance minus the
drawdown threshold), at most 4, decided before each session. Every account starts at 1 micro (before the threshold locks the cushion
is never above $2,500); after it locks at $50,100 that means 2 micros from a balance of $55,100, 3 from $57,600, 4 from $60,100,
and back down when the balance falls (or a payout takes it below). Averages 1.4 micros.
  base (16-year edge):   expected 2-year payouts $3,534 vs $2,950 at a fixed 1 micro (+20%); any payout 59.3% vs 59.4%;
                         open after a year 54.1% vs 54.8%; value per account slot with the 6-micro evaluation $3,001 vs $2,417
  2 ticks a side:        $3,181 vs $2,714
  half the edge:         $1,148 vs $1,107 (no worse) - the deciding scenario
  no edge:               $368 vs $377 (the same small loss)
It keeps fixed 1 micro's reliability and adds most of the upside that only big fixed sizes had (fixed 3: $3,263 but 72% lost before
a payout). Rules that add size sooner (1 per $1,500) pay more at the full edge but lose accounts and money at half the edge - not
eligible. Audit: fixed 1/2/3 micros reproduce biglever.master_qm exactly on all 20,000 paths.

/ $2,500), between 1 and 4; change the strategy's Contracts and accounts.json together when it changes (the strategy refuses to start
when they differ). The evaluation stays at a fixed 6 micros (BIGLEVER/EVALVOL/RISKCTL: shrinking or growing inside the 30 days hurts).
