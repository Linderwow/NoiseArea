PRE-REGISTERED: WIDE SOURCE SWEEP (BEST WEB / PAPERS / CODE / YOUTUBE) + SELF-IMPROVING TEST LOOP, FOR MNQ
Written 2026-10-20 at Sashkin's request ("filter out the absolute best YouTube videos or web sources overall ... have an advanced way to
find winnable results by yourself and self-improve ... don't give up"), before any candidate below has been backtested. The live
strategy does not change.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT FROM THE EARLIER ROUNDS
 - Breadth: four independent scouts (academic papers; quant practitioner sites and books; open-source code and verified records;
   YouTube discovery) each return 30-60 candidates with exact rules and an evidence score 0-10. YouTube transcripts of the best-ranked
   videos are read in the browser (muted). Every candidate is logged in the catalog below, tested or not, with the reason.
 - Depth of data: 1-minute bars for the whole 16 years (NQ 2010-07 .. 2019-05 from the local Databento file; MNQ 2019-05 .. 2026-10-16
   from the three local MNQ files, which extends the earlier 1-minute tests past their 2026-04-09 truncation).
 - Self-improvement in two places: (1) around the most promising candidates, a refinement search that only ever looks at the
   discovery decade and pays for every variant it tries (deflated Sharpe); (2) the search itself adapts - after stage 1-2 the families
   and source types that scored best steer a second scout round, whose candidates go through the same gauntlet (counted in the trials).

DATA, COSTS, FILLS  src2_lib.py. Times New York, bars stamped at their start. Costs as everywhere: 1 tick + $0.50 per side at today's
price (p_ref), returns on the day's 09:30 price (intraday) or entry price (multi-day), quoted per micro at today's notional. Stops and
stop-entries fill at their level (or the open if gapped through), limits only when traded through by a tick, stop and target in the
same minute = stop. A session whose contract changes inside a trade is skipped (intraday) / the trade is closed at the roll (multi-day).
Periods: DISCOVERY NQ 2010-07-01 .. 2019-05-05; VALIDATION MNQ 2019-05-06 .. 2022-12-31; HOLDOUT MNQ 2023-01-01 .. 2026-10-16.

STAGE 1 - AS PUBLISHED (no fitting; defaults exactly as the source states; where the source leaves a choice open the first listed
value below is the default)
 Scored over all 16 years. EDGE needs all of: full-sample t above the Holm-corrected one-sided 5% bar over all stage-1 candidates
 (k = catalog count), and at least 2.50; MNQ 2019-2026 alone t >= 2.0; >= 60% of years positive; t >= 1.5 at 2 ticks a side.
 Also (so plain market exposure cannot pass as an edge - NQ rose about 15x over the sample): alpha t >= 2.0 in a regression of the
 strategy's daily net return on the market's own close-to-close return. Buy-and-hold is shown as a reference row.
 ADDITION (for EDGE strategies only): NoiseArea + candidate daily Sharpe above NoiseArea alone, month-block bootstrap P < 0.01
 (Holm over the EDGE strategies). The post-publication part of each sample is shown separately (it is the cleanest out-of-sample).

STAGE 2 - SELF-IMPROVING REFINEMENT (discovery only)
 The 10 stage-1 candidates with the highest DISCOVERY t (selected mechanically by code, whatever their other numbers) get a
 neighbourhood search on DISCOVERY ONLY: every numeric parameter at x0.5 / x0.75 / x1.25 / x1.5 / x2 of its published value (integers
 rounded, duplicates dropped), every categorical choice the source mentions, one at a time and in pairs; the published version and
 every single change also with each of four filters (entries before 12:00 only / from 12:00 only - intraday rules; only in the
 direction of the 5-session return / only against it). [Amended before any result: the "NoiseArea flat" filter of the first draft
 was replaced by the "against the 5-session return" filter - NoiseArea's minute-level position is not available for the NQ decade.]
 Each variant's discovery Sharpe is deflated (Bailey & Lopez de Prado) by the TOTAL number of stage-2 variants. Survivors (DSR > 0.95;
 the null-simulation audit of RULEMINE showed this bar is conservative) go to VALIDATION: t >= 2.0 same sign, Holm over survivors.
 Validated rules, at most 10, go to the HOLDOUT once: PASS = holdout t >= 2.0 after Bonferroni over them, at 1 and at 2 ticks, and the
 ADDITION test. Nothing that fails is retried on the holdout. Disclosure: stage 1 prints each published version's full-sample figures
 (holdout years included); stage-2 selection is by discovery t only, mechanically, so those figures cannot steer it.

STAGE 3 - ADAPTIVE SEARCH ROUND
 Score each source type and strategy family by the median discovery t of its stage-1 members. The top two families steer a second
 scout round (more variants of those families from the same source types); new candidates are pre-registered here as an amendment
 before testing and go through stages 1-2 with k and N counted cumulatively.

AUDITS  every coded strategy's trade list for 3 random sessions printed and checked by hand against the 1-minute bars; a second,
plain-loop implementation of every EDGE or stage-2 survivor; the deflated-Sharpe function reused from rulemine.py (audited).
FILES  src2_lib.py, src2_test.py -> src2_output.txt, src2_check.py, SOURCES2_CATALOG.txt, SOURCES2_RESULTS.txt.
