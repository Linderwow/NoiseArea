MarketStructure - professional use (2026-11-03)

WHAT IT IS
  Swing-point market structure on any chart: swing highs/lows (N bars each side, confirmed N bars later), the pending levels, and
  every break of them on a bar close - BOS (break of structure, continuation) or CHoCH (change of character, reversal) - with the
  structure state (bullish / bearish) and the levels whose break would change it. Nothing repaints: Calculate is forced to
  OnBarClose, a swing exists only once N later bars confirm it, and a break is a completed bar's close.

INSTALL (yours)
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File deploy.ps1      (refuses while Sim101 holds a position; F5 reloads every script)
  NinjaTrader: New > NinjaScript Editor > F5 > wait for "compiled". Chart > right-click > Indicators > MarketStructure.
  Undo: rollback.ps1 then F5.

SETTINGS
  Swing strength 5, Break on Close - the defaults, and the ones the research graded. On the 5-minute MNQ chart that is a 25-minute
  swing each side, about 13 breaks per session. 10 = fewer, bigger swings (7 a session); 3 = noise (19 a session).
  Draw history 3,000 bars (keeps a long chart fast; 0 = everything). Sound alert off by default; it fires on live bars only.
  Export breaks CSV on: every break goes to agent_exports\MarketStructure_breaks_<instrument>_<period>_<tag>.csv.

TIME FRAME
  Use the 5-minute chart - the strategy's own bars, the one the research graded. The structure is a map at every time frame and a
  forecast at none: 1-minute structure is noise plus costs (following it: t -4 to -6 over 7 years); 15 / 30 / 60-minute breaks
  follow through 47-50% of the time, the same as 5-minute. A longer bar gives fewer breaks, not better ones.

HOW TO READ THE PANEL
  Structure : BULLISH  since 10:35  (3 legs)   - the state and how many breaks in a row made it
  Last break: BOS up 28,310.25 at 11:05
  Above     : 28,355.50 (swing 11:20) -> BOS up        - the level whose break continues the structure
  Below     : 28,240.00 (swing 10:50) -> CHoCH down    - the level whose break flips it
  Solid line = CHoCH, dashed = BOS, green up / red down; dots = confirmed swings; thin hashes = the pending levels.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE SAYS (research\MSB_RESULTS.txt, pre-registered in MSB_PLAN.txt before any figure)
  The direction of a structure break does not predict the next hour on Nasdaq futures: 47-50% follow-through, 0-3 ticks mean move
  before a 4-tick round trip, about $0/yr trading every break on MNQ, losses on MES, at 1, 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes, for BOS and
  CHoCH alike, with close or wick breaks, holding or flipping. Nothing met the bar.
  So: do not trade the arrows, do not put a strategy on Bias/Signal, do not add it to NoiseArea's rules. Use it to see where the
  swings and the levels are, to describe a session after the fact, and to know, in advance, which price would change the read.

FOR STRATEGIES (if you ever want the state in code)
  MarketStructure(5).Bias[0]  (+1 / -1 / 0), .Signal[0] (+2 CHoCH up, +1 BOS up, -1 BOS down, -2 CHoCH down), .StructureHigh[0] /
  .StructureLow[0] (pending levels, NaN when none). Same non-repainting guarantee inside a strategy.

AUDIT TRAIL
  research\msb.py is the same rule in Python, line for line (msb_test.py: 20 hand-made-bar checks, ALL PASS). After the indicator
  has run on a chart:  python research\msb_parity.py agent_exports\MarketStructure_breaks_MNQ_5Minute_chart.csv agent_exports\NoiseArea_bars_MNQ_strategy_Sim101.csv
  must print PARITY PASS - the chart's breaks and the research port's breaks match one for one.
